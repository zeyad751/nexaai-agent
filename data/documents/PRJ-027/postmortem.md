# Post-Mortem: Centralized AI Platform for Grid Analytics
**Date:** February 2024
**Status at close:** Completed 14 days ahead of schedule; actual cost
$2.83M against a $2.84M budget (essentially on budget)

## What Happened
One of the strongest outcomes in the portfolio: revenue reached $4.8M
against a $2.83M investment, adoption climbed to nearly 85%, and
satisfaction reached 8.6/10 by close. The extended pilot validation
period (see October 2022 meeting notes) meant Phase 2's cross-region
rollout began with a model trained on a full seasonal cycle rather than
a single season's data.

## What Worked
- The "extend and validate across a full seasonal cycle before scaling"
  checkpoint mirrors the same pattern used successfully on the Meridian
  Fraud Detection and Delta Steel Predictive Maintenance engagements —
  this is now a recurring, deliberate practice across NexaAI's stronger
  outcomes, not a coincidence specific to any one account.
- Sahara Power's central IT authority over regional operations, an
  explicit precondition in the proposal, held throughout — regional
  teams adopted the shared platform without the resistance seen on
  engagements where that authority is weaker.
- The Energy sector's lighter regulatory review burden (versus Banking,
  Government, or Insurance) meant the delivery plan's lack of built-in
  compliance checkpoints did not create the friction seen on comparable
  Enterprise AI Transformation engagements in those other industries.

## What Could Have Gone Better
Nothing significant flagged.

## Lessons Learned
Reinforces two lessons already emerging elsewhere in the portfolio:
validate across a full relevant cycle (seasonal, in this case) before
scaling a platform strategy, and industry regulatory profile is a larger
driver of Enterprise AI Transformation timeline risk than execution
quality.
