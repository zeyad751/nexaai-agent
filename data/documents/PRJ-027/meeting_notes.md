# Meeting Notes: Grid Analytics Platform — Phase 1 Steering Review
**Date:** October 12, 2022
**Attendees:** Ryan Shawky (NexaAI PM), Faisal Abdel Rahman (NexaAI, AI
Engineering), Sahara Power Group CTO, Sahara Power Regional Operations
Director (pilot region)

## Discussion
The pilot region reported a meaningful drop in undetected grid anomalies
since go-live, with the anomaly-detection model catching several load
irregularities that would previously have gone unnoticed until a
downstream fault. The Regional Operations Director noted this had
already changed how the regional team plans maintenance windows.

The Group CTO, encouraged by the pilot results, asked about compressing
the Phase 2 multi-region rollout timeline. Ryan Shawky recommended
instead extending the pilot region's validation period by one additional
quarter, specifically to accumulate a broader range of seasonal load
patterns before the cross-region model tuning begins in Phase 2 — the
pilot region's data to date covers only one season, and grid load
patterns vary meaningfully across seasons.

## Decision
Approved additional budget to extend pilot validation through an
additional seasonal cycle before beginning Phase 2 — a positive-outcome
decision prioritizing a more complete training signal over an
accelerated multi-region timeline.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to continue pilot-region monitoring through the additional
  seasonal cycle
- Sahara Power to begin Phase 2 regional-readiness planning in parallel,
  without committing to a start date until the extended pilot concludes
