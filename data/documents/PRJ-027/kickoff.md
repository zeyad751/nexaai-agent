# Kickoff Document: Centralized AI Platform for Grid Analytics
**Date:** April 28, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-027

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Sahara Power & Energy, Group CTO
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Ryan Shawky
- **NexaAI AI Engineering Lead:** Faisal Abdel Rahman, AI Engineer Lead

## Objectives
1. Deploy centralized load-forecasting and anomaly-detection capabilities
   across all regional grid operations
2. Establish shared infrastructure enabling cross-region model learning
3. Demonstrate measurable improvement in anomaly detection versus current
   region-by-region tooling

## Initial Team
23-person team, the largest in the current portfolio, spanning AI
Engineering, Data & Analytics, Cloud & Infrastructure, Strategy
Consulting, Finance, and Cybersecurity.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Timeline:** coordinating rollout across multiple regional operations
  teams on a shared platform timeline is a known scheduling complexity;
  flagged for close program management.

## Initial Plan
Phase 1 (2022): platform foundation and first-region pilot. Phase 2
(2023): expansion to remaining regions with cross-region model tuning.
Phase 3 (early 2024): stabilization and handoff.
