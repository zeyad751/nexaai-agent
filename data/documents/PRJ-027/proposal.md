# Project Proposal: Centralized AI Platform for Grid Analytics
**Client:** Sahara Power & Energy
**Date:** February 2022

## Business Problem
Sahara Power & Energy operates grid infrastructure across multiple
regions, each currently running separate, locally-managed analytics
tooling for load forecasting and anomaly detection, with no shared
infrastructure or cross-region learning.

## Proposed Solution
An **Enterprise AI Transformation** engagement establishing a centralized
grid analytics platform: shared load-forecasting and anomaly-detection
capabilities deployed across all regions from a single governed
infrastructure.

## Expected Outcomes
- Consistent grid analytics capability across all regions rather than
  fragmented, locally-built tooling
- Cross-region learning — anomaly patterns detected in one region
  improve detection models for all regions
- A foundation for future grid AI use cases beyond initial forecasting
  and anomaly detection

## Key Assumptions
- Sahara Power's central IT function has clear authority to mandate
  platform adoption across regional operations
- As an Energy-sector account, this program's technical/operational use
  cases do not carry the same compliance review burden that comparable
  Enterprise AI Transformation programs face in Banking, Government, or
  Insurance accounts

## Strategy & Approach
Enterprise AI Transformation is recommended given Sahara Power's
board-level commitment and multi-year budget for grid modernization.
Given the lighter regulatory profile of grid analytics relative to
customer-facing financial or government use cases, the delivery plan
does not build in extended compliance review checkpoints as a structural
element.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $2.75M-$2.9M over an estimated 22-month engagement.
