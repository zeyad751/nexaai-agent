# Project Proposal: Patient Data Governance & Responsible AI Program
**Client:** Al-Waha Healthcare Group
**Date:** September 2022

## Business Problem
Al-Waha lacks a formal governance framework for patient data used in any
current or future AI initiatives, a gap its board wants addressed ahead
of further AI investment.

## Proposed Solution
A **Responsible AI Framework** engagement establishing patient data
governance policies, fairness testing standards, and an AI initiative
approval process.

## Expected Outcomes
- A board-approved patient data governance framework
- A repeatable approval process for future clinical AI initiatives

## Strategy & Approach
Responsible AI Framework is applied given the sensitivity of patient
data and the direct relevance of governance to any future healthcare AI
work at this account.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $390K-$420K over an estimated 6-month engagement.
