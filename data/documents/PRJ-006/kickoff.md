# Kickoff Document: Patient Data Governance & Responsible AI Program
**Date:** November 14, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-006

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Al-Waha Healthcare Group, Chief Medical Information Officer
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Mostafa Rashad

## Objectives
1. Establish a board-approved patient data governance framework
2. Define fairness testing standards for future clinical AI use cases
3. Build a repeatable AI initiative approval process

## Initial Team
Small cross-functional team spanning Strategy Consulting and
Cybersecurity, given the policy/governance nature of this engagement.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: framework design and stakeholder review. Months 4-6:
approval and rollout of the governance process.
