# Project Proposal: Clinical Documentation NLP Pilot
**Client:** Nileside Medical Centers
**Date:** February 2022

## Business Problem
Physicians at Nileside's pilot outpatient department spend significant
time on clinical documentation, time that could otherwise go to direct
patient care.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: an NLP model assisting with clinical note
summarization and structured-field extraction, tested in a single
department before considering wider rollout.

## Expected Outcomes
- Reduced physician documentation time in the pilot department
- A go/no-go basis for evaluating broader network rollout

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot given this is Nileside's first clinical NLP engagement
and organizational fit is untested.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $88K-$100K over an estimated 2-month pilot.
