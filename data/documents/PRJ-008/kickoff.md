# Kickoff Document: Clinical Documentation NLP Pilot
**Date:** April 14, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-008

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Nileside Medical Centers, pilot department Chief Physician
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Khaled Naguib

## Objectives
1. Deploy NLP-assisted documentation for the pilot department
2. Measure documentation time reduction
3. Produce a network-wide rollout recommendation

## Initial Team
Small AI Engineering-led team given the single-department pilot scope.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-3: model development against pilot department note samples.
Weeks 4-8: live use and physician feedback collection.

*(Note: this pilot's strong results directly informed the subsequent
Enterprise Clinical AI Rollout, PRJ-009 — see that project's documents
for how the network-wide scale-up unfolded.)*
