# Project Proposal: Generative AI Customer Support Assistant
**Client:** Nour Telecom
**Date:** March 2024

## Business Problem
Nour Telecom's customer support channels handle high volumes of routine
technical support inquiries that could be resolved without agent
involvement.

## Proposed Solution
A **Product-Led AI** engagement: a generative AI support assistant
treated as an ongoing internal product, with a dedicated Nour Telecom
product owner continuing iteration after NexaAI's initial delivery.

## Expected Outcomes
- Deflection of routine technical support inquiries
- A capability that continues improving after the engagement closes,
  owned internally by Nour Telecom's product team

## Strategy & Approach
Product-Led AI is proposed given Nour Telecom's existing digital product
organization, capable of absorbing long-term ownership of the capability.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $325K-$355K over an estimated 10-month engagement.
