# Kickoff Document: RPA for Procurement Workflows
**Date:** August 19, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-017

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Horizon Auto Components, Head of Procurement
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Daniel Haddad
- **NexaAI Automation Lead:** Ryan Al-Otaibi, Senior AI Engineer

## Objectives
1. Automate purchase order processing for the top standardized
   transaction categories
2. Automate invoice-matching for those same categories
3. Train procurement staff on the new automated workflow and escalation
   process for exceptions

## Initial Team
6-person team, lean given the well-scoped, single-department nature of
the engagement.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Budget:** per-vendor purchase order format variation is a known open
  question pending a format sampling exercise in week one.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-2: purchase order format sampling and automation rule design.
Weeks 3-10: automation build and staff training. Weeks 11-16: pilot
rollout and stabilization.
