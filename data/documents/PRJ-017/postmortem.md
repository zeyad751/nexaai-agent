# Post-Mortem: RPA for Procurement Workflows (Cancelled)
**Date:** October 2022
**Status at close:** Cancelled ~10 weeks into a planned 16-week
engagement; actual cost $54.4K against a $172.3K budget (partial spend
before termination); no revenue recognized

## What Happened
The engagement was cancelled by mutual agreement after procurement staff
did not adopt the automated workflow despite completed training,
continuing to process transactions manually in parallel. Root-caused to
a genuine gap in the original requirements process rather than a
technical failure of the automation itself.

## Root Cause
Procurement staff's actual day-to-day judgment involved substantial
informal, relationship-based adjustments for long-standing vendors —
terms flexibility that does not appear anywhere in formal purchase order
data and that the automation was never designed to handle. This was not
surfaced during requirements-gathering, where the process was described
in more standardized terms than staff actually apply in practice.
Underlying, unstated concern about role security among procurement staff
compounded the resistance once the gap became apparent, but the technical
gap was the root trigger.

## What Could Have Gone Better
Requirements-gathering sessions were conducted primarily with
procurement leadership rather than directly observing or shadowing
front-line staff's actual transaction handling. Leadership's description
of "standardized" purchase order processing did not match the informal
adjustments staff routinely made in practice.

## Lessons Learned
For any Automation-First engagement targeting a workflow with
longstanding, relationship-based client accounts (vendors, in this case;
could equally be customer accounts elsewhere), include direct
front-line-staff shadowing during requirements-gathering, not just
leadership interviews — the gap between "how leadership describes the
process" and "how staff actually execute it" is exactly where adoption
risk lives, and it will not surface in a standard requirements document.
