# Executive Review: RPA for Procurement Workflows (Cancelled)
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** November 2022
**Client:** Horizon Auto Components | **Strategic Importance:** Low

## Summary
Cancelled after roughly two-thirds of the planned timeline, with limited
financial exposure ($54.4K spent against a $172.3K budget) given the
early termination. The root cause — front-line staff's informal,
relationship-based workflow adjustments not surfacing during
leadership-level requirements gathering — is a specific, addressable
process gap rather than a strategy or technology failure.

## Management Observations
This is a useful, low-cost lesson relative to what a similar gap could
have cost on a larger Automation-First engagement. Worth noting the
engagement was Low complexity and caught relatively early — the same
failure mode on a High-complexity, multi-department automation
engagement would be materially more expensive to unwind.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend adding front-line staff shadowing as a standard requirements-
gathering step for any Automation-First proposal, not an optional
addition — this is now a documented cause of a full cancellation, not a
hypothetical risk.

## Open Items
Horizon has not indicated interest in revisiting automation; account
relationship remains open for other engagement types.
