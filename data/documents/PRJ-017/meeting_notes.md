# Meeting Notes: RPA Procurement — Adoption Concerns Escalation
**Date:** September 22, 2022
**Attendees:** Daniel Haddad (NexaAI PM), Ryan Al-Otaibi (NexaAI, AI
Engineering), Horizon Head of Procurement, Horizon procurement team leads

## Discussion
Five weeks into training and pilot rollout, Daniel Haddad reported that
procurement staff usage of the automated workflow remained minimal —
staff were continuing to process purchase orders manually alongside the
automated system rather than transitioning to it, effectively doubling
effort rather than reducing it.

When pressed on the reason, procurement team leads were candid: staff
were uncomfortable trusting the automation's invoice-matching accuracy
for vendor relationships they considered "high-touch" (long-standing
suppliers with informal, relationship-based terms adjustments that don't
appear in the formal purchase order data), and several staff had raised
informal concerns about what full automation would mean for their roles
longer-term — concerns that had not surfaced during the original
requirements-gathering sessions.

The Head of Procurement acknowledged that leadership had underestimated
how much of the team's actual day-to-day judgment involved these
informal, relationship-based adjustments that the automation was not
designed to handle, and how much that shaped staff resistance beyond a
simple training or change-management gap.

## Discussion Continued — Commercial Terms
NexaAI proposed a scope reduction — automating only the subset of
vendors with no history of informal terms adjustments — as a way to
salvage adoption. Horizon's Head of Procurement indicated this would
reduce the addressable transaction volume so much that the original
commercial terms no longer made sense for either side to continue under.

## Decision
Both parties agreed to terminate the engagement rather than continue
under revised, much-reduced scope and terms. Logged as a negative-outcome
decision — the underlying adoption issue was not something either party
could resolve within the committed budget and timeline.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to document a post-mortem on the informal-terms-adjustment gap
  for future procurement-automation proposals
- Horizon to close out the engagement with no further automation work
  planned at this time
