# Project Proposal: RPA for Procurement Workflows
**Client:** Horizon Auto Components
**Date:** June 2022

## Business Problem
Horizon Auto Components' procurement team manually processes purchase
orders and vendor invoice matching, a high-volume, repetitive workflow
that the procurement leadership has identified as a strong automation
candidate.

## Proposed Solution
An **Automation-First** engagement: RPA-based automation of purchase
order processing and invoice-matching workflows, targeting the highest-
volume, most standardized transaction categories first.

## Expected Outcomes
- Reduced manual processing time for standard purchase orders and
  invoice matching
- Fewer manual-entry errors in vendor payment processing
- A foundation for expanding automation to additional procurement
  workflows over time

## Key Assumptions
- Procurement staff will adopt the automated workflow for the targeted
  transaction categories once trained
- Standard purchase order formats are consistent enough across vendors
  to automate without extensive per-vendor customization

## Strategy & Approach
Automation-First is proposed given the high transaction volume and
repetitive nature of the target workflows — a strong fit profile for
this strategy based on NexaAI's experience in Manufacturing and
Logistics accounts.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $165K-$180K over an estimated 4-month engagement.
