# Project Proposal: Generative AI Product Descriptions Tool
**Client:** Cedar Retail Group
**Date:** August 2024

## Business Problem
Cedar Retail's product catalog team manually writes product descriptions
for thousands of SKUs, a slow process that delays new product listings.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: a generative AI tool drafting product descriptions
from structured product attributes, tested on a subset of the catalog.

## Expected Outcomes
- Faster product description drafting versus manual writing
- A go/no-go basis for full-catalog rollout

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot given the value of testing content quality on a subset
before full-catalog investment.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $155K-$170K over an estimated 4-month pilot.
