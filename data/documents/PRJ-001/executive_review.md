# Executive Review: Fraud Detection AI Platform
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** March 2024
**Client:** Meridian National Bank | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
One of NexaAI's strongest banking-sector delivery outcomes to date. The
engagement closed on schedule, slightly under budget, with strong and
still-climbing adoption (over 90% by close) and the highest customer
satisfaction score (8.8/10) of any project on the Meridian account. The
Responsible AI Framework overhead, often a point of client pushback on
other regulated-industry engagements, was well received here — Meridian's
model risk committee specifically credited the governance documentation
for accelerating their internal review cycles.

## Management Observations
Ryan Shawky (PM), Dina Sabry (Senior AI Engineer), Priya Saleh (Senior
Data Scientist), and Ali Al-Sabah (Senior Cloud Engineer) have now
delivered strong outcomes together across multiple engagements.
Leadership should consider how to protect this group's availability for
other strategically important banking-sector pursuits, rather than

## Strategic Implications
This engagement is a strong reference case for positioning the
Responsible AI Framework as an accelerant rather than pure overhead in
regulated-industry proposals — the extended-pilot-before-scale pattern
used here is worth formalizing into our standard banking-sector
methodology guidance.
