# Meeting Notes: Fraud Detection AI Platform — Steering Committee
**Date:** February 15, 2023
**Attendees:** Ryan Shawky (NexaAI PM), Amr Hassan (NexaAI, Director of AI
Engineering), Meridian Chief Risk Officer's delegate, Head of Fraud
Operations (Meridian)

## Discussion
The Head of Fraud Operations opened by noting that the pilot channel
(digital banking) has been running in production for two full quarters
and analyst feedback has been strongly positive — false-positive volume
is down noticeably, and the risk-score explanations are being used
directly in analyst case notes, which was not originally scoped but has
become one of the most-cited benefits internally.

Amr Hassan presented the model performance trend and flagged that
adoption within the fraud operations team is still ramping — several
senior analysts have fully switched to the new scoring workflow, but a
portion of the team is still cross-checking against the legacy rules
engine out of caution. The team agreed this is expected during a
transition period and not a cause for concern.

The Meridian CRO delegate raised the question of extending the pilot
phase on the digital channel by one additional quarter before beginning
the retail-channel rollout, specifically to build a larger production
track record ahead of the retail rollout's model risk committee review —
retail carries materially higher transaction volume and the committee has
indicated it will want to see a longer stability window.

## Decision
The steering committee approved additional budget to extend the pilot
phase before proceeding to full rollout, rather than moving to retail on
the original timeline. Rationale: the extra production track record is
expected to streamline the retail-channel committee review, and the
digital-channel results to date justify the additional investment.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to prepare an extended pilot performance report for the Q2 2023
  model risk committee session
- Meridian to nominate two additional fraud analysts to shadow the new
  workflow ahead of the retail rollout
