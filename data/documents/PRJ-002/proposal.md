# Project Proposal: Core Banking Cloud Migration
**Client:** Meridian National Bank
**Date:** November 2022

## Business Problem
Meridian's core banking systems run on aging on-premise infrastructure
that limits scalability for the bank's growing digital banking
initiatives, including the Fraud Detection AI Platform delivered in
2022-2024.

## Proposed Solution
A **Cloud-First AI** engagement: migrating core banking infrastructure
to Azure/Kubernetes ahead of further AI capability expansion.

## Expected Outcomes
- Scalable infrastructure removing the on-premise capacity ceiling
- Faster deployment cycles for future AI and digital banking capabilities

## Key Assumptions
- Core banking migration can proceed on a comparable regulatory review
  timeline to the infrastructure work already completed under the Fraud
  Detection engagement

## Strategy & Approach
Cloud-First AI is proposed to establish scalable infrastructure ahead of
further AI investment, given Meridian's continued digital banking
roadmap.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $1.7M-$1.8M over an estimated 17-month engagement.
