# Executive Review: Core Banking Cloud Migration
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** September 2024
**Client:** Meridian National Bank | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
Completed ~51 days behind schedule with actual cost $2.41M against a
$1.73M budget (~40% over) — one of the larger overruns in the portfolio.
Root cause: a third-party cloud networking vendor's integration
component was repeatedly delayed, a dependency the migration's low-
traffic-window scheduling could not work around since the vendor
component sat on the critical path for cutover itself, not just a
peripheral feature.

## Management Observations
This is now a second Vendor dependency-driven overrun on the Meridian
account (technology vendor, not a compliance issue this time), separate
from the AI-output-compliance-scope pattern seen on PRJ-003. Satisfaction
declined slightly over the engagement (5.4 to 4.5), consistent with a
client experiencing repeated vendor-driven schedule slips outside
NexaAI's direct control but still affecting their perception of the
overall engagement.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend that any infrastructure migration proposal identify third-party
components on the technical critical path explicitly at proposal stage,
with a contingency plan (alternate vendor, fallback architecture) rather
than treating vendor delivery risk as a generic, monitored-but-unplanned
risk.

## Open Items
None outstanding; migration is live and stable as of close.
