# Kickoff Document: Core Banking Cloud Migration
**Date:** January 23, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-002

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Meridian National Bank, Head of IT Infrastructure
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Hanan Sabry, Project Manager Lead

## Objectives
1. Migrate core banking infrastructure to Azure/Kubernetes
2. Maintain zero unplanned downtime for live banking services during
   migration
3. Establish scalable foundation for future AI capability expansion

## Initial Team
16-person team, primarily Cloud & Infrastructure, with Cybersecurity
support given the sensitivity of core banking systems.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** migrating live core banking infrastructure without
  service interruption is inherently complex; flagged for phased,
  low-traffic-window migration scheduling.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-6: migration architecture and non-critical system pilot.
Months 7-15: phased core system migration. Months 16-17: validation and
stabilization.
