# Project Proposal: Warehouse Automation (Computer Vision + RPA)
**Client:** Redline Logistics
**Date:** February 2023

## Business Problem
Redline's warehouse inventory counting and sorting processes remain
largely manual, following the success of the Route Optimization Engine's
data-driven approach in fleet operations.

## Proposed Solution
An **Automation-First** engagement: computer-vision-based inventory
counting combined with RPA-based sorting-workflow automation.

## Expected Outcomes
- Faster, more accurate inventory counting
- Reduced manual sorting workflow time

## Strategy & Approach
Automation-First is proposed given the high-volume, repetitive nature of
warehouse counting and sorting tasks.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $300K-$330K over an estimated 5-month engagement.
