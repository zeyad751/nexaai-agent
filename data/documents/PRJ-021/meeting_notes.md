# Meeting Notes: Logistics Data Platform — Discovery Findings Escalation
**Date:** April 15, 2024
**Attendees:** Karim Youssef (NexaAI PM), Ali Bishop (NexaAI, Data &
Analytics), Redline Group CIO, Redline Regional Operations Directors
(x2)

## Discussion
Ali Bishop presented findings from the warehouse-level data sampling
conducted across a representative subset of regional sites during weeks
1-4 of discovery — the verification step the proposal had explicitly
flagged as abbreviated at proposal stage. The results diverged sharply
from the "largely standardized following the 2021 upgrade" description
given during proposal discussions: only 2 of 5 sampled regional
warehouses had actually completed the 2021 WMS standardization in
practice; the remaining sites were still running customized, pre-upgrade
configurations with materially different data structures.

One Regional Operations Director acknowledged, somewhat uncomfortably,
that the 2021 initiative had been reported as "complete" at the
corporate level for board reporting purposes once a majority of sites
had begun rollout, but that several regional sites' actual technical
migration had stalled for local operational reasons never escalated back
to corporate IT.

The Group CIO acknowledged the gap between the corporate-reported status
and the ground-truth regional reality, and asked what this meant for the
committed timeline.

## Decision
The team agreed to expand the data cleansing and governance workstream
to cover the non-standardized regional sites individually, rather than
treating the WMS data as uniform. Logged as a negative-outcome decision:
this directly reflects the abbreviated proposal-stage verification the
team had explicitly flagged as a risk, now materialized.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to produce a revised timeline and budget reflecting per-site
  remediation for the 3 non-standardized warehouses
- Redline corporate IT to establish a direct verification channel with
  regional sites going forward, rather than relying on rollout-status
  self-reporting
