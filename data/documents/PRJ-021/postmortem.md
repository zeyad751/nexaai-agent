# Post-Mortem: Centralized Logistics Data Platform
**Date:** August 2025
**Status at close:** Completed behind the (already-revised) plan; actual
cost $3.21M against a $2.35M budget (~37% over) — the largest cost
overrun percentage in the current portfolio

## What Happened
This engagement's outcome traces directly to a compromise made explicit
at proposal stage: an abbreviated data maturity verification (a
documentation review rather than technical sampling), accepted to meet
Redline's compressed proposal timeline ahead of a board reporting cycle.
Discovery-phase sampling in April 2024 revealed the client's own
corporate-level reporting of WMS standardization did not reflect the
actual state at 3 of 5 regional sites sampled.

## Root Cause
Distinct from the Delta Steel Manufacturing case (PRJ-015), where
NexaAI generalized from an unrelated prior engagement's success, this
failure traces to the client's own internal reporting being inaccurate
— not maliciously, but because a corporate initiative was marked
"complete" once rollout had *begun* at most sites, without verifying
regional completion. NexaAI's proposal explicitly flagged the abbreviated
verification as a scoping compromise rather than a completed check, which
is the correct process — but the compromise was accepted under timeline
pressure rather than declined or resolved with a paid discovery phase
first, which had been recommended and was deferred by the client.

## What Worked
The explicit written flag in the proposal document meant there was no
ambiguity about where the risk sat once it materialized — the
conversation with the client centered on remediation, not on
disagreement about whether NexaAI had done its due diligence.

## Lessons Learned
When a client defers a recommended paid discovery/verification phase
under timeline pressure, and the resulting data maturity assumption is
sourced from the client's own self-reported status, that should be
treated as a higher-risk assumption than a NexaAI-conducted-but-
abbreviated assessment — self-reported corporate status is subject to
its own reporting incentives (e.g., board-reporting deadlines) that can
diverge from ground truth in ways a client's own team may not fully
realize until directly sampled.
