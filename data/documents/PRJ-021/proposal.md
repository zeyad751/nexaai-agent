# Project Proposal: Centralized Logistics Data Platform
**Client:** Redline Logistics
**Date:** December 2023

## Business Problem
Redline Logistics operates warehouse management, fleet tracking, and
customer order systems that are not currently integrated, limiting
visibility into end-to-end shipment performance.

## Proposed Solution
A **Data-First Transformation**: a governed Databricks/Snowflake data
platform consolidating warehouse management (WMS), fleet tracking, and
order data ahead of future logistics AI use cases.

## Key Assumptions
Redline's IT leadership, under a board-mandated digital modernization
initiative, represented their WMS data as "largely standardized across
our regional warehouses following a 2021 system upgrade" during proposal
discussions. This proposal's timeline and budget are built on that
representation. NexaAI's standard practice is to independently verify
data maturity claims through direct technical sampling; given the
compressed proposal timeline requested by Redline (to align with a board
reporting cycle), this verification was abbreviated to a documentation
review rather than a full technical sample of warehouse-level data. We
are noting this explicitly as a scoping compromise, not a completed
verification.

## Expected Outcomes
- A consolidated logistics data platform across warehouse, fleet, and
  order systems
- Foundation for future shipment-performance and route-optimization AI
  use cases

## Strategy & Approach
Data-First Transformation is proposed on the strength of the
represented 2021 WMS standardization. We recommend, but Redline has
deferred, an initial paid discovery phase that would have independently
verified this claim before committing to the full engagement timeline.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $2.2M-$2.4M over an estimated 17-month engagement.
