# Executive Review: Centralized Logistics Data Platform
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** August 2025
**Client:** Redline Logistics | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
The largest cost overrun percentage (~37%) in the current portfolio,
traced to a client-side data maturity misrepresentation (corporate-
reported WMS standardization that did not reflect regional reality) on
top of a proposal-stage verification compromise that NexaAI explicitly
flagged but accepted under the client's timeline pressure.

## Management Observations
This is now the third distinct flavor of the same underlying pattern
across the portfolio: STRAT-03 (Data-First Transformation) engagements
fail specifically when the client's data maturity is not what was
assumed, and the *source* of that wrong assumption varies — NexaAI's own
unverified generalization (Delta Steel, PRJ-015), or, as here, the
client's own inaccurate self-reported status. In both failure cases, the
mitigation is the same: independently sample the actual systems in
scope regardless of the source or confidence of the maturity claim.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend that any proposal where a client defers a recommended
verification/discovery phase under timeline pressure carry an explicit,
quantified budget contingency reflecting that specific, named risk —
rather than proceeding on the client's preferred timeline with the risk
noted but not financially hedged.

## Open Items
Redline's Group CIO has asked for a joint review of the corporate-vs-
regional reporting gap that caused this, independent of NexaAI's own
process review; scheduled for Q4 2025.
