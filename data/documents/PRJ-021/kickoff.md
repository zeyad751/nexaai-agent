# Kickoff Document: Centralized Logistics Data Platform
**Date:** February 28, 2024
**Project ID:** PRJ-021

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Redline Logistics, Group CIO
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Karim Youssef
- **NexaAI Data & Analytics Lead:** Ali Bishop, Data Scientist Lead

## Objectives
1. Consolidate WMS, fleet tracking, and order data into a governed
   platform
2. Validate the represented 2021 WMS standardization across all regional
   warehouses during technical discovery
3. Establish a foundation for future logistics AI use cases

## Initial Team
12-person team, primarily Data & Analytics and Cloud & Infrastructure,
with a Strategy Consultant added specifically to support the governance
documentation workstream.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** the proposal's abbreviated data maturity verification
  (documentation review rather than technical sampling, per the
  proposal's explicit note) is flagged as the primary risk to validate
  early in discovery.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-4: technical discovery, explicitly including warehouse-level
data sampling across a representative subset of regional sites — the
verification step abbreviated at proposal stage. Months 2-12: platform
build. Months 13-17: migration and governance handoff.
