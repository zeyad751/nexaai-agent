# Kickoff Document: Cloud Migration for SCADA Data Systems
**Date:** January 4, 2024
**Project ID:** PRJ-028

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Sahara Power & Energy, Group CTO
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Hanan Sabry, Project Manager Lead
- **NexaAI Cloud Lead:** Sherif Shawky, Cloud Engineer Lead

## Objectives
1. Migrate SCADA historian data to Azure/Kubernetes-based cloud
   infrastructure
2. Maintain operational continuity for live grid monitoring throughout
   migration
3. Establish a foundation for future analytics use cases beyond current
   grid analytics scope

## Initial Team
21-person team, primarily Cloud & Infrastructure and Data & Analytics,
with Strategy Consulting support for change management, carrying forward
several senior team members from the grid analytics program.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Timeline:** SCADA systems' closer proximity to live operational
  control (versus the analytics-layer scope of the prior engagement) is
  noted as a scheduling consideration; flagged for early stakeholder
  alignment with plant operations teams.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-4: migration architecture and pilot on a single non-critical
data stream. Months 5-12: phased migration across remaining SCADA data
streams. Months 13-16: validation and stabilization.
