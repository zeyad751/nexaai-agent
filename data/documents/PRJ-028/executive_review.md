# Executive Review: Cloud Migration for SCADA Data Systems
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** August 2025
**Client:** Sahara Power & Energy | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
A genuine "poor start, strong recovery" case: overall project score
climbed from 34.8 to 63.1 across the engagement after a sharp early
stall driven by an IT/OT authority conflict, resolved through a
structural governance pivot (formal operations sign-off) rather than a
technical fix.

## Management Observations
This is a distinct and valuable lesson from the other recovery cases in
the portfolio (Nileside Clinical AI, Falcon Motors Marketing Engine):
the failure here was organizational-authority-shaped, not data- or
technical-quality-shaped. Worth adding IT/OT distinction awareness
specifically to any future proposal touching industrial control or
similarly operationally-sensitive systems, even for an account where
NexaAI has prior, successful delivery history.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend a standing proposal-stage question for any infrastructure or
cloud migration engagement: "does this system sit in a reporting/
analytics layer, or a live operational control layer?" — the answer
should directly determine whether operations, not just IT, holds formal
governance authority in the delivery plan from day one.

## Open Items
None outstanding; Sahara Power has not yet indicated further SCADA-
adjacent migration plans.
