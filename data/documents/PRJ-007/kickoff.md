# Kickoff Document: Back-Office Claims Automation
**Date:** May 12, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-007

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Al-Waha Healthcare Group, Head of Claims Operations
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Bilal Baxter, Director of Project Management
- **NexaAI Automation Lead:** Amr Hassan, Director of AI Engineering

## Objectives
1. Automate end-to-end processing for defined routine claim categories
2. Establish escalation rules for any claim outside automation confidence
   thresholds
3. Deliver an auditable log of every automated decision for compliance
   review

## Initial Team
14-person team spanning AI Engineering (automation logic), Cloud &
Infrastructure (RPA platform hosting), and Project Management, with
senior PM oversight given the healthcare-adjacent compliance
considerations flagged at proposal stage.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** defining "routine" claim criteria with sufficient
  precision to avoid both over- and under-automation is a known
  implementation challenge; flagged for iterative refinement during
  build.

## Initial Plan
Month 1: claim category definition and escalation-rule design with
Claims Operations. Months 2-3: automation build. Months 4-5: pilot on a
limited claim category set, then expand to full target scope.
