# Project Proposal: Customer Churn Prediction
**Client:** Nour Telecom
**Date:** December 2021

## Business Problem
Nour Telecom's customer retention team currently relies on broad
demographic segments to target retention offers, without a
predictive view of which specific customers are at highest churn risk.

## Proposed Solution
A **Customer-Centric AI** engagement: a churn prediction model
identifying at-risk customers for targeted retention outreach.

## Expected Outcomes
- Improved retention-offer targeting versus current broad segmentation
- Measurable reduction in customer churn rate among targeted segments

## Strategy & Approach
Customer-Centric AI is proposed given the customer-experience-led nature
of the retention use case.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $560K-$610K over an estimated 6-month engagement.
