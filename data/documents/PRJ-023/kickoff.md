# Kickoff Document: Customer Churn Prediction
**Date:** February 14, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-023

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Nour Telecom, Head of Customer Retention
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Hanan Sabry, Project Manager Lead

## Objectives
1. Deploy a churn prediction model identifying at-risk customers
2. Integrate predictions into the retention team's outreach workflow
3. Measure churn rate improvement among targeted segments

## Initial Team
Data Science-led team with Product support for retention workflow
integration.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: model development against historical churn data. Months 4-6:
retention workflow integration and live measurement.

*(Note: this project's success directly informed Nour Telecom's decision
to commit to the broader Enterprise AI Transformation Program, PRJ-024.)*
