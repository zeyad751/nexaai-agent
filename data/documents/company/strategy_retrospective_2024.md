# NexaAI Solutions: 2022-2024 Strategy Performance Retrospective
**Document type:** Internal strategy review
**Prepared by:** Strategy Consulting practice
**Date:** December 2024

## Purpose
A qualitative retrospective on how NexaAI's core strategic approaches
have performed across the 2022-2024 project portfolio, intended to
inform proposal-stage strategy selection going forward. This is a
high-level synthesis; see individual project documents for engagement-
specific detail.

## Observations by Strategy

**Data-First Transformation** performs reliably when the client's data
maturity assessment is genuinely verified at the systems level before
committing — not merely inferred from organizational reputation, a
related but different system's track record, or the client's own
self-reported status. Every underperforming engagement under this
strategy traces to a maturity assumption that was not independently
verified; every strong outcome traces to one that was.

**Enterprise AI Transformation** shows a clear, consistent
industry-conditioned performance split: engagements in Telecommunications
and Energy have significantly outperformed, on schedule and budget
adherence, comparable-complexity engagements in Banking, Government, and
Insurance. Technical execution quality has been comparable across
industries; the differentiator is each industry's compliance review
burden, which regulated-industry proposals should budget for structurally
rather than as a monitored risk.

**Automation-First** continues to perform strongly in Manufacturing and
Logistics. Healthcare-adjacent applications require materially more
careful category-by-category compliance review — "administrative" and
"requires clinical judgment" are not always cleanly separable at the
individual-workflow level in that vertical.

**Rapid AI Pilot** consistently produces high client satisfaction, but
a follow-on conversion rate lower than other strategies — client
feedback collected across pilot engagements attributes this primarily to
external client budget-cycle and governance-process timing rather than
pilot performance. Worth setting realistic follow-on-conversion
expectations at proposal stage.

**A cross-cutting pattern, not specific to any one strategy**: several
of the portfolio's stronger engagements share a specific delivery
practice — extending a pilot or initial validation phase before
committing to full-scale rollout, even when early results are already
positive. This has consistently produced better full-scale outcomes than
scaling immediately on early signal. Recommend formalizing this as a
named checkpoint in delivery methodology rather than leaving it to
individual project managers to apply where they judge it useful.

## Recommendation
Incorporate the industry-conditioned Enterprise AI Transformation finding
and the systems-level verification requirement for Data-First
Transformation into standard proposal review checklists.
