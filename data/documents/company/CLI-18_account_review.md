# Client Account Review: Gulf Public Services Authority
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** October 2025
**Account Manager:** Sales team (EMP-084) | **Relationship since:** July 2022
**Strategic Priority:** High

## Account History
Two engagements to date:

| Project | Start | Revenue | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smart Government Services Platform | 2022-09 | $1.26M (over cost) | Completed - Delayed |
| Predictive Resource Allocation | 2024-03 | $381K | Completed |

## Financial Observation
The flagship platform engagement ran over cost (see PRJ-036 executive
review — a vendor-dependency-driven overrun compounded by public-sector
procurement constraints on vendor substitution), and the follow-on
engagement, while profitable, was substantially smaller in scope and
revenue. Financial performance on this account has been the weaker of
NexaAI's two Government relationships to date.

## Why This Account Remains High Priority
1. **UAE public-sector reference**: alongside the Ministry of Digital
   Development account (Egypt), this gives NexaAI reference presence in
   government accounts across two GCC/MENA markets rather than one.
2. **Lessons already applied**: the Predictive Resource Allocation
   engagement was deliberately scoped under Incremental Modernization
   specifically to avoid repeating the vendor-dependency exposure from
   the first engagement — an internal signal that the account team is
   actively managing this relationship's risk profile, not simply hoping
   for a better outcome next time.
3. **Shorter but growing relationship**: at just over three years, this
   relationship is younger than the Ministry of Digital Development
   account; strategic priority here reflects growth potential more than
   an established track record.

## Recommendation
Maintain strategic priority, but recommend the next proposal for this
account include an explicit vendor-substitution contingency plan (per
the PRJ-036 lessons learned) before committing to another large,
vendor-dependent platform engagement.
