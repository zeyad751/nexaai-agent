# Client Account Review: Ministry of Digital Development
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** October 2025
**Account Manager:** Sales team (EMP-080) | **Relationship since:** March 2019
**Strategic Priority:** High

## Account History
NexaAI's longest-standing government relationship, predating the current
project portfolio by nearly three years. Three engagements to date:

| Project | Start | Revenue | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Digital Services AI Roadmap | 2022-01 | $3.89M | Completed |
| Citizen Service Chatbot (Arabic NLP) | 2023-06 | $790K | Completed |
| Government Data Platform Modernization | 2024-09 | $395K (partial, in progress) | In Progress |

## Financial Observation
Deal size has declined noticeably across the relationship — from a
$3.89M flagship roadmap engagement to progressively smaller, more
narrowly-scoped follow-on projects. This is not a sign of a weakening
relationship; it reflects the natural shape of a phased public-sector
digital transformation roadmap, where the large first engagement
establishes shared infrastructure and governance that subsequent,
smaller phases build on rather than re-purchase.

## Why This Account Remains High Priority Despite Modest Recent Revenue
Three reasons, discussed at the most recent account review:
1. **Sector reference-ability**: this is NexaAI's flagship government
   account in Egypt, and is cited in proposals to other public-sector
   prospects.
2. **Sustained relationship depth**: six years of continuous engagement
   (since 2019) with a single government client is a credibility signal
   in a sector where vendor relationships are typically shorter and more
   transactional.
3. **Pipeline visibility**: the Ministry has indicated interest in
   further phases beyond the current Data Platform Modernization
   engagement, which the account team expects to materialize once that
   engagement's compliance review (see PRJ-035 documents) concludes.

## Recommendation
Continue treating this as a strategic (not purely revenue-based) account
priority. Recommend against measuring this account's success primarily
on a per-project revenue basis, given the deliberate phased-and-narrowing
engagement shape.
