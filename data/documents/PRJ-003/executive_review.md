# Executive Review: AI-Powered Customer Service Chatbot
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** May 2025
**Client:** Meridian National Bank | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
Delivered, but with a schedule and cost overrun (~54 days, ~24% over
budget) that leadership should be aware of when scoping future
generative-AI, customer-facing work on regulated-industry accounts. The
underlying cause — treating a new AI output type's compliance review
scope as equivalent to a previous, differently-shaped engagement on the
same account — is a pattern risk worth flagging beyond this one project.

## Management Observations
Customer satisfaction (around 5/10 throughout) and adoption (climbing but
still under 50% by close) both landed weaker than the Fraud Detection
project's figures, consistent with a rollout that spent much of its
timeline in compliance remediation rather than user-facing iteration.
Financially, ROI turned positive only in the final two quarters.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend a standing proposal-stage checklist item for any new
generative/customer-facing use case on an account with prior NexaAI
delivery history: explicitly confirm compliance review scope with the
client's Legal function rather than inferring it from a prior, differently
-shaped engagement. This is now the second account (see also the STRAT-05
Healthcare automation pattern) where an underestimated compliance review
was the primary driver of overrun.

## Open Items
Meridian's Customer Experience team has asked about extending the
assistant to additional inquiry categories; recommend scoping that as a
distinct proposal with compliance review as an explicit, separately
budgeted workstream from day one.
