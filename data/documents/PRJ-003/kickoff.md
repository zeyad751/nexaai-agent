# Kickoff Document: AI-Powered Customer Service Chatbot
**Date:** March 3, 2024
**Project ID:** PRJ-003

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Meridian National Bank, Head of Customer Experience
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Hady Adeyemi
- **NexaAI Strategy Lead:** Tarek Nabil, Senior Strategy Consultant
- **NexaAI Data & Analytics Lead:** Mostafa Farouk, Director of Data & Analytics

## Objectives
1. Launch a generative-AI assistant covering the top 20 routine inquiry
   categories by ticket volume
2. Integrate with the existing service ticketing system for seamless
   hand-off to human agents when needed
3. Establish a reusable retrieval infrastructure pattern for future
   customer-facing AI capabilities

## Initial Team
15-person team spanning AI Engineering, Data & Analytics, Strategy
Consulting, Product, and Project Management. Notably lighter on
Cybersecurity/Compliance representation than the Fraud Detection
engagement, reflecting an initial assumption that customer-facing
generated text carries a materially different (and lighter) compliance
profile than automated fraud decisions.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Timeline:** the retrieval infrastructure buildout and legal review of
  generated-response guardrails are both new territory for this account;
  flagged as a scheduling risk to monitor.

## Initial Plan
Phase 1 (Q2 2024): retrieval infrastructure and core conversational
design. Phase 2 (Q3-Q4 2024): pilot on a subset of inquiry categories.
Phase 3 (Q1 2025): full rollout across all 20 target categories.
