# Project Proposal: AI-Powered Customer Service Chatbot
**Client:** Meridian National Bank
**Date:** December 2023

## Business Problem
Meridian's customer service call center has seen a sustained rise in
ticket volume for routine account inquiries (balance checks, transaction
disputes, card replacement requests) that do not require a human agent
but currently consume first-line agent capacity.

## Proposed Solution
A generative-AI-powered customer service assistant, built on a commercial
LLM API layered with a retrieval system over Meridian's product and
policy documentation, deployed across the mobile app and web banking
portal. This is proposed as part of a broader **Enterprise AI
Transformation** engagement, positioning the chatbot as the first
customer-facing capability under Meridian's multi-year AI roadmap
(following the Fraud Detection AI Platform delivered in 2022-2024).

## Expected Outcomes
- Deflect a meaningful share of routine inquiries away from first-line agents
- Provide 24/7 coverage for common account questions
- Establish reusable conversational-AI infrastructure for future
  customer-facing use cases under the broader transformation roadmap

## Key Assumptions
- Existing product/policy documentation is sufficiently structured for
  retrieval-based grounding with moderate cleanup effort
- Compliance requirements for this use case are comparable in scope to
  the Fraud Detection engagement's governance work, given both fall under
  the same Responsible AI umbrella
- Legal review of customer-facing generated responses can proceed in
  parallel with technical development

## Strategy & Approach
Delivered under Enterprise AI Transformation given its role as a
flagship, multi-phase capability within Meridian's broader roadmap rather
than a standalone pilot. Scope includes conversational design, retrieval
infrastructure, and integration with the existing service ticketing
system.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $1.8M-$1.9M over an estimated 12-month engagement.
