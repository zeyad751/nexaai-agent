# Project Proposal: Smart Government Services Platform
**Client:** Gulf Public Services Authority
**Date:** July 2022

## Business Problem
Gulf Public Services Authority's citizen service portal is built on
aging infrastructure that limits the Authority's ability to introduce
new digital services quickly.

## Proposed Solution
An **Enterprise AI Transformation** engagement: a modernized citizen
services platform with integrated AI capabilities for service request
triage and processing.

## Expected Outcomes
- A modernized, AI-integrated citizen services platform
- Faster service request processing versus current infrastructure

## Key Assumptions
- Technology vendor components required for the platform integration
  are available on a timeline consistent with the proposed schedule

## Strategy & Approach
Enterprise AI Transformation is recommended given the Authority's
board-level digital modernization mandate and multi-year budget
commitment.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $900K-$970K over an estimated 14-month engagement.
