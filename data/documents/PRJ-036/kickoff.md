# Kickoff Document: Smart Government Services Platform
**Date:** September 13, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-036

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Gulf Public Services Authority, Digital Services Director
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Khaled Naguib

## Objectives
1. Deliver a modernized citizen services platform with integrated AI
   triage capabilities
2. Reduce service request processing time versus current infrastructure
3. Establish a foundation for future digital service expansion

## Initial Team
15-person team spanning AI Engineering, Cloud & Infrastructure, and
Strategy Consulting.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Budget:** the platform integration depends on a third-party
  technology vendor component; flagged as a dependency to track closely
  given public-sector procurement timelines for any vendor substitution
  if needed.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-6: platform architecture and vendor component integration.
Months 7-12: AI triage capability build. Months 13-14: rollout and
stabilization.
