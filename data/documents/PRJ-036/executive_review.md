# Executive Review: Smart Government Services Platform
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** February 2024
**Client:** Gulf Public Services Authority | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
Completed ~60 days behind schedule with actual cost $1.33M against a
$936K budget (~42% over) — the largest overrun percentage among the
Enterprise AI Transformation engagements. Root cause: the third-party
technology vendor component flagged as a dependency at kickoff was
delayed, and public-sector procurement rules limited how quickly an
alternate vendor could be substituted once the delay became apparent.

## Management Observations
This is now a second Vendor dependency-driven overrun (alongside PRJ-002,
a technology vendor case rather than a compliance one), and specifically
a case where the risk was correctly flagged at kickoff but the
mitigation option (vendor substitution) was constrained by the client's
own procurement rules in ways the delivery plan hadn't accounted for.
Satisfaction declined modestly (5.4 to 4.9), consistent with the
extended, vendor-driven delay.

## Strategic Implications
For public-sector engagements specifically, recommend confirming vendor-
substitution procurement timelines explicitly at proposal stage for any
critical-path third-party component — a private-sector client's ability
to quickly swap vendors does not transfer to a public-sector account
bound by formal procurement processes.

## Open Items
Platform is live and stable as of close; no further phases currently
scoped with this account.
