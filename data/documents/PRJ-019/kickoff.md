# Kickoff Document: Route Optimization Engine
**Date:** May 12, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-019

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Redline Logistics, Head of Fleet Operations
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Aya Fahmy

## Objectives
1. Deploy an iterative route optimization model
2. Reduce fuel usage and delivery time versus static routing
3. Refine the model based on real delivery-performance feedback each cycle

## Initial Team
Data & Analytics-led team with Cloud & Infrastructure support for
real-time routing integration.

## Initial Plan
Iterative build-measure-learn cycles across the engagement, with initial
deployment on a subset of routes.
