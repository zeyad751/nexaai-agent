# Project Proposal: Route Optimization Engine
**Client:** Redline Logistics
**Date:** March 2022

## Business Problem
Redline Logistics' route planning relies on static routing rules that
don't adapt to real-time traffic or delivery volume fluctuations,
resulting in suboptimal fuel usage and delivery times.

## Proposed Solution
A **Build-Measure-Learn** engagement: an iterative route optimization
model, refined based on real delivery-performance feedback.

## Expected Outcomes
- Reduced fuel usage and delivery time versus static routing
- A model that improves iteratively based on real operational feedback

## Strategy & Approach
Build-Measure-Learn is proposed given Redline's openness to iterative
delivery and the value of real operational feedback in tuning
route-optimization logic.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $335K-$365K over an estimated 10-month engagement.
