# Kickoff Document: Generative AI Marketing Content Engine
**Date:** September 9, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-040

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Falcon Motors Group, Head of Marketing
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Khaled Naguib
- **NexaAI AI Engineering Lead:** Amr Hassan, Director of AI Engineering

## Objectives
1. Deploy a generative content engine covering product descriptions,
   social posts, and promotional copy
2. Establish a feedback loop for iterative content quality improvement
3. Measurably reduce marketing team time spent on first-draft content

## Initial Team
13-person team spanning AI Engineering, Data & Analytics, and Product,
with a Product Manager included specifically to own the feedback-loop
design given the Build-Measure-Learn approach.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** brand voice consistency across models and markets is a
  known open question for generative content at this scale; flagged for
  early iteration.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: initial content engine build and first-draft generation
pipeline. Months 4-6: marketing team feedback cycles and iteration.
Months 7-9: broader rollout across models and markets.
