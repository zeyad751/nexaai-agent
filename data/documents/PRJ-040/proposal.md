# Project Proposal: Generative AI Marketing Content Engine
**Client:** Falcon Motors Group
**Date:** July 2023

## Business Problem
Falcon Motors' marketing team manually writes product descriptions,
social media copy, and promotional content for each vehicle model and
regional market, a process that does not scale well across the group's
growing number of models and markets.

## Proposed Solution
A **Build-Measure-Learn** engagement: a generative AI content engine
producing draft marketing copy across product descriptions, social
posts, and promotional material, iterated based on real usage and
marketing team feedback.

## Expected Outcomes
- Significantly faster content production across models and markets
- A content engine that improves iteratively based on real marketing
  team usage rather than a single upfront specification
- Reduced marketing team time spent on first-draft content creation

## Key Assumptions
- Marketing team is comfortable with an iterative, imperfect-early-
  versions approach consistent with Build-Measure-Learn, rather than
  expecting a fully specified deliverable from day one

## Strategy & Approach
Build-Measure-Learn is proposed given the inherently iterative nature of
brand voice and content quality — we do not expect to fully specify
"what good marketing copy sounds like" upfront, and plan to converge on
it through real usage and feedback cycles rather than exhaustive initial
specification.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $580K-$620K over an estimated 9-month engagement.
