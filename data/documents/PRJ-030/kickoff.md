# Kickoff Document: Claims Fraud Detection
**Date:** July 8, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-030

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Trustline Insurance Group, Head of Claims
  Investigation
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Aya Fahmy
- **NexaAI AI Engineering Lead:** Ryan Al-Otaibi, Senior AI Engineer

## Objectives
1. Deploy a fraud-likelihood scoring model replacing the current static
   rules engine
2. Reduce false-positive claim flags reaching investigator review
3. Measurably reduce confirmed fraud losses within the engagement window

## Initial Team
14-person team, primarily AI Engineering and Data & Analytics, with
senior Project Management leadership from Bilal Baxter and Hanan Sabry
supporting Aya Fahmy.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Budget:** model training data volume and quality for the
  confirmed-fraud-case category (a smaller dataset than the overall
  claims volume) is a known open question pending initial data
  assessment.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: model development against historical claims data. Months
4-5: pilot scoring alongside the existing rules engine. Month 6: full
cutover to model-based scoring.
