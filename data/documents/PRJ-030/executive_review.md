# Executive Review: Claims Fraud Detection
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** February 2023
**Client:** Trustline Insurance Group | **Strategic Importance:** Medium

## Summary
A financially excellent outcome (ROI settling at 69.5%, revenue $875K
against $516K invested) paired with the weakest end-user satisfaction
trend of any completed engagement (declining from 4.2 to 3.6 out of 10).
The gap is real and traceable to a specific proposal-stage assumption
that investigator experience "would not be a significant factor" — an
assumption that was never validated and did not hold.

## Management Observations
This is a useful counter-example to keep alongside our strongest
satisfaction-and-ROI-aligned engagements: strong financial performance
does not guarantee end-user acceptance, and the two can diverge sharply
when a business case is built entirely around one dimension without
checking the other. The client's decision to accept the friction rather
than fund a fix was reasonable given the numbers, but reflects a design
gap, not a client preference.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend a lightweight end-user interface/workflow validation step
(even a short session with 2-3 actual end users) become standard
practice for any engagement whose business case explicitly assumes a
stakeholder group's experience is a secondary concern — catching this
kind of gap during design costs far less than living with it post-launch.
