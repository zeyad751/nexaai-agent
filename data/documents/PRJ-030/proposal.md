# Project Proposal: Claims Fraud Detection
**Client:** Trustline Insurance Group
**Date:** May 2022

## Business Problem
Trustline's claims fraud investigation team currently reviews flagged
claims using a static rules engine with a high false-positive rate,
consuming investigator time on claims that turn out to be legitimate.

## Proposed Solution
A machine-learning-based fraud scoring model, delivered under NexaAI's
**Responsible AI Framework** given the direct financial and customer
impact of fraud-related claim decisions, replacing the static rules
engine with a model that ranks claims by fraud likelihood.

## Expected Outcomes
- Reduced false-positive rate versus the current rules engine
- Faster investigator triage through fraud-likelihood scoring
- Measurable reduction in confirmed fraud losses

## Key Assumptions
- Trustline's historical claims and confirmed-fraud-case data is
  sufficient for supervised model training
- Investigators will incorporate model scores into their existing
  triage workflow with reasonable training

## Strategy & Approach
Responsible AI Framework is applied given the direct impact of
fraud-related decisions on claimants. The business case is built
primarily around fraud-loss reduction and investigator efficiency —
a financial and operational metric — rather than an explicit
investigator-satisfaction target, which we do not anticipate being a
significant factor for a model designed to reduce investigators'
false-positive review burden.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $510K-$550K over an estimated 6-month engagement.
