# Executive Review: Enterprise Clinical AI Rollout
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** February 2024
**Client:** Nileside Medical Centers | **Strategic Importance:** High

## Summary
A genuine "poor start, strong recovery" case worth studying as a
positive example of mid-project strategy correction. The project scored
among the weakest in the portfolio at its Q2 2023 checkpoint (overall
score 38.7) and finished among the stronger outcomes on the Healthcare
vertical (67.0), with satisfaction, adoption, and ROI all climbing
substantially after the Q2 2023 strategy pivot from a custom-model
approach to Product-Led AI.

## Management Observations
The recovery was not merely "things got better over time" — it traces to
a specific, identifiable decision (documented in the April 2023 meeting
notes and formalized as two logged decisions: the original approach
marked negative-outcome, the pivot marked positive-outcome). This is a
cleaner causal story than most recovery cases and worth using in internal
training on when and how to recognize a failing approach early enough to
pivot rather than push through.

## Strategic Implications
The specific lesson — that a successful pilot's data characteristics
should not be assumed representative without an explicit cross-population
sampling check — applies beyond clinical NLP and should be added to
NexaAI's standard pilot-to-scale playbook.

## Open Items
Nileside's internal clinical informatics owner (named as part of the
pivot) has expressed interest in extending the retrieval-based approach
to inpatient documentation; not yet scoped as a formal proposal.
