# Kickoff Document: Enterprise Clinical AI Rollout
**Date:** February 19, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-009

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Nileside Medical Centers, Chief Medical Information Officer
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Khaled Naguib
- **NexaAI AI Engineering Lead:** Ola El-Sayed, AI Engineer Lead

## Objectives
1. Train a network-wide clinical documentation NLP model covering all
   outpatient departments
2. Deploy centrally with a single maintained model rather than
   per-department variants
3. Achieve documentation time reduction comparable to the pilot
   department's results

## Initial Team
11-person team, primarily AI Engineering and Data & Analytics, carrying
forward continuity from the pilot's technical approach.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** scaling a model trained on one department's note
  patterns to network-wide diversity is untested; flagged as the primary
  technical risk to monitor during early training runs.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-2: network-wide data aggregation and initial model training.
Months 3-6: department-by-department validation and iteration. Months
7-10: full rollout and stabilization.
