# Executive Review: AI Governance & Responsible AI Program
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** November 2024
**Client:** Trustline Insurance Group | **Strategic Importance:** High

## Summary
Delivered and board-approved, but with a significant overrun (~35% over
budget, 53 days late) driven by the same "assumed compliance scope
parity" failure mode now seen on a second account (Meridian National
Bank, PRJ-003). This is no longer an isolated incident — it is a
recognizable, recurring proposal-stage risk across regulated-industry
accounts.

## Management Observations
Combined with PRJ-030 (same client, prior engagement), Trustline is now
a account with both a "strong ROI, weak satisfaction" data point and a
"regulatory scope underestimated" data point — worth a dedicated account
review considering both patterns together before the next Trustline
proposal.

## Strategic Implications
Firmly recommend elevating "verify new compliance/governance scope
independently, even on an account with prior compliance work" from a
lessons-learned note to a mandatory proposal-stage gate for any
regulated-industry AI governance or compliance-adjacent engagement.

## Open Items
Trustline's CRO has asked NexaAI to propose the next AI use case under
the newly-approved framework; not yet scoped.
