# Kickoff Document: AI Governance & Responsible AI Program
**Date:** August 2, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-031

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Trustline Insurance Group, Chief Risk Officer
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Mostafa Rashad
- **NexaAI Strategy Lead:** Tarek Nabil, Senior Strategy Consultant

## Objectives
1. Establish a company-wide AI governance framework covering model risk,
   fairness testing, and approval processes
2. Produce regulatory-ready documentation for the UAE insurance
   regulator
3. Extend Trustline's existing risk and compliance function to cover
   AI-specific governance

## Initial Team
19-person team, primarily Strategy Consulting and Cybersecurity, with
AI Engineering support for technical fairness-testing standards.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** translating regulatory model-risk language into
  concrete, testable technical standards is a known area of ambiguity;
  flagged for early legal/technical alignment sessions.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-4: framework design and regulatory alignment sessions. Months
5-9: fairness-testing standards and approval-process build. Months
10-12: board approval and rollout across business lines.
