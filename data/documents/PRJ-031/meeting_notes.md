# Meeting Notes: AI Governance Program — Regulatory Scope Escalation
**Date:** November 8, 2023
**Attendees:** Mostafa Rashad (NexaAI PM), Tarek Nabil (NexaAI,
Strategy), Trustline Chief Risk Officer, Trustline external regulatory
counsel

## Discussion
Trustline's external regulatory counsel, engaged to review the draft
governance framework, informed the team that the UAE insurance
regulator's expectations for a *company-wide, board-approved* AI
governance framework are materially more extensive than what was
required for the Claims Fraud Detection engagement's model-specific
compliance review — the earlier review covered one model's fairness and
explainability; a company-wide framework requires documented governance
covering every current and future AI use case's risk classification,
which is a different order of documentation and review.

Tarek Nabil acknowledged the proposal had assumed rough parity between
the two compliance scopes, and that this was now shown to be inaccurate.

The Chief Risk Officer, while frustrated at the schedule implication,
noted that getting this right mattered more than hitting the original
timeline, given the framework would govern all future AI initiatives
Trustline pursues.

## Decision
The team agreed to insert a formal, extended regulatory review gate
before finalizing the framework for board approval — logged as a
mixed-outcome decision: correct and necessary, but a direct cost to the
original schedule stemming from the inaccurate scope-parity assumption.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to expand the framework documentation to cover risk
  classification for all current and anticipated future AI use cases
- Trustline regulatory counsel to provide a detailed review checklist
  ahead of the next draft submission
