# Project Proposal: AI Governance & Responsible AI Program
**Client:** Trustline Insurance Group
**Date:** June 2023

## Business Problem
Following the 2022 Claims Fraud Detection deployment and growing
interest in additional AI use cases across underwriting and customer
service, Trustline's Board has asked for a formal, company-wide AI
governance framework ahead of approving further AI initiatives.

## Proposed Solution
An **Enterprise AI Transformation** engagement establishing a
company-wide AI governance framework: model risk policies, fairness
testing standards, and an approval process for future AI initiatives
across all Trustline business lines.

## Expected Outcomes
- A documented, board-approved AI governance framework
- A repeatable model approval process for future AI initiatives
- Regulatory-ready documentation for the UAE insurance regulator's
  model risk expectations

## Key Assumptions
- Trustline's existing risk and compliance function can be extended to
  cover AI-specific governance without requiring an entirely new
  organizational structure
- Regulatory review requirements for this governance framework are
  comparable in scope to the compliance work already completed for the
  Claims Fraud Detection engagement

## Strategy & Approach
Enterprise AI Transformation is applied given the board-level,
company-wide scope of this initiative. Given this program's direct
subject matter is regulatory compliance itself, we anticipate but have
not fully scoped the possibility that the regulator's expectations for a
formal governance framework may exceed what a single prior
model-specific compliance review (Claims Fraud Detection) already
covered.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $1.55M-$1.65M over an estimated 12-month engagement.
