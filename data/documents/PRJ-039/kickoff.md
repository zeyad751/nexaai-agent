# Kickoff Document: Predictive Maintenance for Dealership Network
**Date:** December 9, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-039

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Falcon Motors Group, Group Head of Aftersales
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Karim Youssef

## Objectives
1. Deploy predictive maintenance scoring at participating dealerships
2. Support business-unit-level customization of the underlying model
3. Measure service scheduling accuracy improvement

## Initial Team
Data & Analytics-led team with a lightweight central coordination
function, consistent with the Business-Unit-Led AI model.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: shared model foundation development. Months 4-6:
dealership-level onboarding and customization.
