# Project Proposal: Predictive Maintenance for Dealership Network
**Client:** Falcon Motors Group
**Date:** October 2022

## Business Problem
Falcon Motors' dealership service centers operate independently with no
shared predictive maintenance capability for identifying vehicles likely
to need service soon based on usage patterns.

## Proposed Solution
A **Business-Unit-Led AI** engagement: each dealership business unit
funds and adopts a predictive maintenance capability tailored to its own
customer base, with light central coordination on the underlying model.

## Expected Outcomes
- Improved service scheduling accuracy at participating dealerships
- Faster adoption through business-unit-level ownership and buy-in

## Strategy & Approach
Business-Unit-Led AI is proposed given Falcon Motors' decentralized
dealership network, where individual business units control their own
technology budgets and customer relationships.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $570K-$620K over an estimated 6-month engagement.
