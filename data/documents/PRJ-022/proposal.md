# Project Proposal: Delivery ETA Prediction Pilot
**Client:** Cargo Bridge Egypt
**Date:** July 2022

## Business Problem
Cargo Bridge currently provides customers with static, route-distance-
based delivery estimates that do not account for real traffic or
seasonal demand patterns, leading to frequent estimate misses.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: a predictive ETA model incorporating historical
traffic and demand data, tested against a subset of delivery routes.

## Expected Outcomes
- Improved ETA accuracy versus the current static estimate method
- A go/no-go basis for broader rollout across all routes

## Key Assumptions
- Sufficient historical delivery and traffic data exists for the pilot
  route subset to support model training

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot given the value of validating accuracy improvement on a
limited route set before any larger commitment.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $120K-$135K over an estimated 2-month pilot.
