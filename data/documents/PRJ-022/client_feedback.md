# Client Feedback: Delivery ETA Prediction Pilot
**Client:** Cargo Bridge Egypt
**Collected:** November 2022, end-of-pilot review

## Feedback Summary
"Customers noticed the difference — fewer complaints about missed
delivery windows on the pilot routes. We'd genuinely like to expand this
to all routes eventually."
— Head of Operations, Cargo Bridge Egypt

## Additional Notes
The Head of Operations was candid that Cargo Bridge is a smaller
operation with limited discretionary technology budget in a given year,
and that a company-wide rollout would need to compete with other
planned capital investments (additional delivery vehicles was mentioned
specifically). No commitment was made to a rollout timeline.

## NexaAI Internal Note
Strong satisfaction result (7.5 rising to 8.4) on a low-complexity,
low-budget pilot. The lack of follow-on reflects the client's overall
capital budget constraints as a smaller logistics operator, not pilot
performance.
