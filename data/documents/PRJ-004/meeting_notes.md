# Meeting Notes: Credit Risk Data Foundation — Technical Inventory Readout
**Date:** July 8, 2022
**Attendees:** Khaled Naguib (NexaAI PM), Mostafa Farouk (NexaAI, Data &
Analytics), Falcon Gulf Head of Credit Risk, Falcon Gulf Data Governance
lead

## Discussion
Mostafa Farouk presented findings from the two-week technical inventory
conducted at project start. The results were consistent with the
proposal-stage data maturity assessment: source systems were confirmed
to have reasonably complete lineage documentation, and the bank's
existing Data Governance function — while not yet covering credit risk
specifically — had clear, well-maintained data ownership records that
the team could extend rather than build from scratch.

The Falcon Gulf Data Governance lead noted this was somewhat unusual in
their experience with external consulting engagements — most vendors
assume a "greenfield" data governance state and spend the first month
rediscovering ownership structures that, in this case, already existed
and just needed to be extended into a new domain.

The Head of Credit Risk asked whether this meant the timeline could be
compressed further. The team agreed not to change the committed
timeline, noting that the early inventory results de-risk the *plan*
but do not yet de-risk *execution* — actual data quality issues typically
surface once real migration work begins, not during an inventory phase.

## Decision
No formal decision logged at this session; the team proceeded on the
original 6-month plan as scoped, treating the inventory results as
confirmation rather than grounds for acceleration.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to begin foundation build against the confirmed source-system list
- Falcon Gulf Data Governance lead to formally extend ownership records
  to cover the credit risk domain in parallel
