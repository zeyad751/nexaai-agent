# Kickoff Document: Enterprise Data Foundation for Credit Risk Modeling
**Date:** June 21, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-004

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Falcon Gulf Bank, Head of Credit Risk
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Khaled Naguib
- **NexaAI Data & Analytics Lead:** Mostafa Farouk, Director of Data & Analytics

## Objectives
1. Consolidate credit-risk-relevant data sources into a governed
   Snowflake foundation
2. Establish data quality and lineage documentation extending the bank's
   existing governance function into the credit risk domain
3. Reduce the quarterly model refresh cycle from its current multi-week
   manual reconciliation process

## Initial Team
11-person team, primarily Data & Analytics with Cloud & Infrastructure
support for the Snowflake environment setup, plus senior Project
Management continuity from Khaled Naguib and Daniel Haddad.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Budget:** integration effort across the identified source systems has
  some estimation uncertainty pending a full technical inventory in the
  first two weeks; flagged for early tracking.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-2: full source-system technical inventory to confirm the data
maturity assessment's findings. Months 2-4: data foundation build.
Months 5-6: migration of the first model refresh cycle onto the new
foundation and validation against the legacy process.
