# Kickoff Document: Enterprise AI Transformation Program
**Date:** September 4, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-005

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Falcon Gulf Bank, Group CTO (board-level program owner)
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Khaled Naguib

## Objectives
1. Deliver coordinated AI capabilities across risk, operations, and
   customer analytics
2. Establish shared program governance across previously independent
   business units
3. Maintain sustained board-level sponsorship across the multi-year
   timeline

## Initial Team
16-person team spanning AI Engineering, Data & Analytics, and Cloud &
Infrastructure.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** coordinating delivery across business units with
  historically independent technology decisions is flagged as the
  primary program-level risk, per the proposal's explicit watch item.

## Initial Plan
Phase 1 (2023-2024): risk and operations use cases. Phase 2 (planned):
customer analytics expansion, contingent on Phase 1 governance model
proving effective across units.
