# Executive Review: Enterprise AI Transformation Program
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** January 2025
**Client:** Falcon Gulf Bank | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
Completed ~69 days behind schedule with actual cost $3.76M against a
$2.76M budget (~36% over). Root cause materialized exactly as the
proposal's watch item anticipated: business units historically used to
independent technology decisions resisted the coordinated program
governance model, slowing cross-unit alignment on shared infrastructure
decisions throughout delivery.

## Management Observations
This is a case where the risk was correctly identified at proposal
stage but not converted into a specific mitigation plan — the proposal
named the concern but the delivery plan did not include a concrete
structural response to it (e.g., a phased unit-by-unit onboarding
sequence, or named unit-level governance liaisons). Satisfaction
declined slightly over the engagement (5.5 to 5.0), consistent with
sustained cross-unit friction throughout rather than a single resolved
incident.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend that any proposal explicitly naming an organizational-
coordination risk also include a concrete structural mitigation in the
delivery plan itself, not just a monitoring flag — naming a risk without
a corresponding plan did not prevent it from materializing here.

## Open Items
Phase 2 (customer analytics) is under internal review at Falcon Gulf
pending a governance model reassessment; not yet scoped as committed
work.
