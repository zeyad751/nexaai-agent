# Project Proposal: Enterprise AI Transformation Program
**Client:** Falcon Gulf Bank
**Date:** July 2023

## Business Problem
Following the 2022 Credit Risk Scoring Model engagement (PRJ-004... see
also the Enterprise Data Foundation, PRJ-004 in the internal
numbering — the naming here follows Falcon Gulf's own program
terminology), Falcon Gulf's board has approved a broader multi-year AI
transformation program spanning risk, operations, and customer
analytics.

## Proposed Solution
An **Enterprise AI Transformation** engagement establishing a
multi-year, board-sponsored AI capability roadmap across Falcon Gulf's
risk, operations, and customer analytics functions.

## Expected Outcomes
- Multiple production AI capabilities delivered under a single
  coordinated program
- Sustained board-level sponsorship across the multi-year timeline

## Key Assumptions
- Falcon Gulf's board-level sponsorship will remain stable and
  operationally engaged (not just budget-approving) across business
  units with historically independent technology decision-making

## Strategy & Approach
Enterprise AI Transformation is recommended given board-level budget
commitment. We note Falcon Gulf's business units have historically
operated with meaningful technology-decision autonomy, which is flagged
as a watch item for a strategy that depends on sustained, coordinated
sponsorship across units.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $2.7M-$2.85M over an estimated 15-month first phase.
