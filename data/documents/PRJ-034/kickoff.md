# Kickoff Document: Citizen Service Chatbot (Arabic NLP)
**Date:** June 13, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-034

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Ministry of Digital Development, Deputy Minister
  for Digital Transformation
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Sultan Mansour, Junior Project Manager

## Objectives
1. Deploy an Arabic-language NLP chatbot for routine citizen inquiries
2. Ensure data processing remains within Ministry-controlled
   infrastructure via open-source LLM deployment
3. Reduce wait times for routine service inquiries

## Initial Team
AI Engineering team with open-source LLM deployment experience.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: open-source LLM fine-tuning for Arabic citizen service
language patterns. Months 4-7: pilot and full deployment.
