# Project Proposal: Citizen Service Chatbot (Arabic NLP)
**Client:** Ministry of Digital Development
**Date:** April 2023

## Business Problem
Following the National Digital Services AI Roadmap (PRJ-033), citizens
contacting Ministry service lines in Arabic face longer wait times than
the roadmap's phased plan aims to address in its second increment.

## Proposed Solution
A **Responsible AI Framework** engagement: an Arabic-language NLP
chatbot for routine citizen service inquiries, built on open-source LLMs
to support the Ministry's data sovereignty requirements.

## Expected Outcomes
- Reduced wait times for routine Arabic-language citizen inquiries
- A second successful increment under the broader digital services roadmap

## Strategy & Approach
Responsible AI Framework applies given the citizen-facing, public-sector
nature of this capability; open-source LLMs are used specifically to
keep data processing within Ministry-controlled infrastructure.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $630K-$680K over an estimated 7-month engagement.
