# Project Proposal: Automated Quality Inspection (Computer Vision)
**Client:** Delta Steel Manufacturing
**Date:** April 2024

## Business Problem
Delta Steel's finished-product quality inspection remains manual on
several production lines that have not yet adopted computer vision
inspection, despite strong results from earlier AI engagements on this
account.

## Proposed Solution
An **Automation-First** engagement: computer-vision-based surface defect
detection deployed on the remaining manual-inspection production lines.

## Expected Outcomes
- Consistent, automated defect detection across all production lines
- Reduced reliance on manual visual inspection

## Strategy & Approach
Automation-First is proposed given Delta Steel's high-volume, repetitive
production environment — a strong fit profile per NexaAI's Manufacturing
sector experience.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $310K-$340K over an estimated 7-month engagement.
