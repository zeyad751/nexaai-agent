# Kickoff Document: Predictive Resource Allocation
**Date:** March 15, 2024
**Project ID:** PRJ-037

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Gulf Public Services Authority, Digital Services Director
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Bilal Baxter, Director of Project Management

## Objectives
1. Deploy predictive staff allocation for the first phase of service
   centers
2. Validate allocation accuracy against actual demand patterns
3. Expand incrementally to additional service centers based on Phase 1
   results

## Initial Team
Data & Analytics-led team, moderate size for the phased rollout approach.

## Initial Plan
Phase 1 (months 1-4): model development and pilot at two service
centers. Phase 2 (months 5-8): incremental expansion based on Phase 1
validation.
