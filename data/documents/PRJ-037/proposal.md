# Project Proposal: Predictive Resource Allocation
**Client:** Gulf Public Services Authority
**Date:** December 2023

## Business Problem
Following the Smart Government Services Platform (PRJ-036), the
Authority wants to extend predictive capabilities to staff and resource
allocation across service centers, which currently relies on fixed
historical staffing patterns.

## Proposed Solution
An **Incremental Modernization** engagement: a predictive resource
allocation model deployed in small, low-risk increments across service
centers.

## Expected Outcomes
- Improved staff allocation matching predicted service demand
- A low-risk, phased rollout consistent with public-sector continuity
  requirements

## Strategy & Approach
Incremental Modernization is proposed given the mission-critical nature
of citizen service center staffing and the Authority's preference for
low-disruption rollout following lessons from the prior platform
engagement's vendor-driven delay.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $265K-$290K over an estimated 8-month engagement.
