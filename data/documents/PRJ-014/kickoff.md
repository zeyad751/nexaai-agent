# Kickoff Document: Predictive Maintenance System
**Date:** January 12, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-014

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Delta Steel Manufacturing, Head of Plant Operations
- **Client platform sponsor:** Delta Steel Group CIO (for the
  Centralized AI Platform mandate)
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Ryan Shawky
- **NexaAI AI Engineering Lead:** Faisal Abdel Rahman, AI Engineer Lead

## Objectives
1. Deploy failure-risk prediction models against the pilot plant's
   rolling mill sensor data
2. Establish the shared platform architecture other plants will
   eventually onboard onto
3. Demonstrate measurable reduction in unplanned downtime at the pilot
   plant within the engagement window

## Initial Team
14-person team spanning AI Engineering, Data & Analytics, and Cloud &
Infrastructure, with senior Project Management continuity from Ryan
Shawky.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Budget:** platform-architecture work (build once, reuse across
  plants) versus pilot-specific work is not always a clean split
  estimate-wise; flagged for early tracking.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-4: sensor data pipeline and model development at the pilot
plant. Months 5-7: pilot deployment and validation against real
maintenance events. Months 8-10: platform hardening for
multi-plant onboarding readiness.
