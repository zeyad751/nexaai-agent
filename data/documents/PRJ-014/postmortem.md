# Post-Mortem: Predictive Maintenance System
**Date:** November 2022
**Status at close:** Completed 9 days ahead of schedule; actual cost
$472.7K against a $487.6K budget (under budget)

## What Happened
The pilot delivered strong, clearly attributable results — model
early-warning flags prevented multiple unplanned downtime events during
the extended validation period, which built the confidence needed for
the Group CIO to commit budget for platform hardening ahead of
multi-plant onboarding.

## What Worked
- The decision to extend pilot validation by one quarter before locking
  model thresholds (see August 2022 meeting notes) meant the platform
  handed off to multi-plant onboarding with a genuinely validated
  false-positive/false-negative profile, not just early promising signal.
- Maintenance staff adoption was organic rather than mandated — the
  dashboard's value was evident quickly enough that staff incorporated it
  into daily routine without a formal change-management push.

## What Could Have Gone Better
Platform-architecture effort versus pilot-specific effort (flagged as a
budget risk at kickoff) was harder to cleanly separate in practice than
estimated, though it stayed within acceptable variance and did not
threaten the budget.

## Lessons Learned
For Centralized AI Platform engagements specifically, building in an
explicit "extend and validate before you scale" checkpoint — rather than
scaling immediately on early positive signal — produced a stronger
platform handoff than moving faster would have. Worth formalizing as
standard practice for multi-plant/multi-business-unit platform rollouts.
