# Meeting Notes: Predictive Maintenance — Pilot Extension Discussion
**Date:** August 2, 2022
**Attendees:** Ryan Shawky (NexaAI PM), Faisal Abdel Rahman (NexaAI, AI
Engineering), Delta Steel Head of Plant Operations, Delta Steel Group CIO

## Discussion
The Head of Plant Operations reported that the pilot plant had avoided
two unplanned downtime events in the prior six weeks based directly on
the model's early-warning flags — both cases where maintenance
historically would have only responded after failure. Adoption among
maintenance staff has grown quickly as a result; the team noted staff are
now actively checking the dashboard each shift rather than needing to be
reminded.

The Group CIO asked whether the multi-plant onboarding timeline could be
pulled forward given this early traction, since two additional plants
had already expressed informal interest after hearing about the pilot
results internally.

Ryan Shawky recommended extending validation at the pilot plant by one
additional quarter before committing to a multi-plant rollout schedule,
specifically to build a larger sample of confirmed early-warning
outcomes (both true positives and any false alarms) before the model's
thresholds are locked in for plants NexaAI has less direct visibility
into.

## Decision
The Group CIO approved the additional budget to extend the pilot
validation phase before committing to the full multi-plant rollout
schedule — a positive-outcome decision, prioritizing validation rigor
over speed given the platform's intended reuse across multiple plants.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to compile a formal false-positive/false-negative analysis from
  the extended pilot period
- Delta Steel CIO's office to begin informal readiness conversations with
  the two interested plants without committing to a timeline yet
