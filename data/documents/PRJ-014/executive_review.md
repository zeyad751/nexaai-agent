# Executive Review: Predictive Maintenance System
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** December 2022
**Client:** Delta Steel Manufacturing | **Strategic Importance:** High

## Summary
A clean, strong outcome — delivered early, under budget, with adoption
climbing to 89% and satisfaction to 8.6/10 by close. This is the pilot
phase of what Delta Steel's Group CIO intends as a multi-plant platform;
NexaAI should expect follow-on onboarding proposals for at least two
additional plants (see August 2022 meeting notes).

## Management Observations
The "extend the pilot, validate rigorously, then scale" pattern used here
mirrors what worked on the Meridian Fraud Detection engagement (PRJ-001).
Both engagements deliberately traded a faster nominal timeline for a
stronger evidence base before committing to a wider rollout, and both
outperformed as a result. Worth watching whether this generalizes as a
repeatable pattern across other Centralized AI Platform and multi-phase
rollout engagements.

## Strategic Implications
Delta Steel's central IT authority over plant-level technology decisions
was an explicit precondition in this proposal for recommending Centralized
AI Platform, and it held throughout — a useful reference case for
qualifying future Centralized AI Platform proposals against that same
precondition.
