# Project Proposal: Predictive Maintenance System
**Client:** Delta Steel Manufacturing
**Date:** November 2021

## Business Problem
Delta Steel's rolling mill equipment experiences unplanned downtime
events that are currently addressed reactively — maintenance teams
respond after a failure rather than anticipating it, resulting in
production line stoppages that are costly both in lost output and
emergency repair costs.

## Proposed Solution
A predictive maintenance system using sensor data from mill equipment,
built as the first capability on a **Centralized AI Platform** intended
to eventually serve multiple Delta Steel plants and use cases from a
single shared infrastructure and governance model.

## Expected Outcomes
- Early warning of equipment failure risk ahead of unplanned downtime
- A reusable data and model platform other Delta Steel plants can onboard
  onto for future AI use cases, rather than each plant building its own
- Reduced emergency maintenance costs relative to the current reactive model

## Key Assumptions
- Delta Steel's central IT function has sufficient authority to mandate
  platform adoption across plants once proven at the pilot plant
- Sensor data quality at the pilot plant is representative of what other
  plants can provide

## Strategy & Approach
Centralized AI Platform is proposed specifically because Delta Steel's
central IT and data governance function already has real authority across
plants — a precondition we assess as necessary for this strategy to work,
since centralized platforms tend to struggle where business units operate
with high autonomy and can resist centrally-imposed tooling.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $460K-$500K over an estimated 10-month engagement,
starting at the primary rolling mill plant.
