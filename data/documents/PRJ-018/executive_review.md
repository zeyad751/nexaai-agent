# Executive Review: Enterprise Data Foundation for Supply Chain AI
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** August 2024
**Client:** Horizon Auto Components | **Strategic Importance:** Medium

## Summary
One of the strongest outcomes on the Horizon account — delivered early,
under budget, with adoption reaching 89.5% and satisfaction 8.9/10. Worth
reading alongside the 2022 cancelled procurement automation engagement on
the same account: this recovers the relationship and demonstrates the
earlier cancellation was specific to that engagement's front-line
adoption dependency, not a broader account relationship problem.

## Management Observations
The proposal for this engagement explicitly named the prior cancellation
and explained why this engagement's risk profile was different — worth
noting as a good practice for re-engaging an account after an earlier
engagement ended poorly, rather than proceeding as if the prior
engagement hadn't happened.

## Strategic Implications
Confirms the lesson from the Delta Steel Manufacturing contrast case:
Data-First Transformation performs well when the maturity assessment is
done properly, regardless of the account's history with other,
structurally different engagement types.

## Open Items
Horizon's Supply Chain Planning team has expressed interest in a future
predictive supply chain phase building on this foundation; not yet
scoped as a formal proposal.
