# Kickoff Document: Enterprise Data Foundation for Supply Chain AI
**Date:** October 19, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-018

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Horizon Auto Components, Head of Supply Chain Planning
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Daniel Haddad
- **NexaAI Cloud & Data Lead:** Adam Rizk, Director of Cloud & Infrastructure

## Objectives
1. Consolidate inventory, supplier, and production-scheduling data into
   a governed Snowflake foundation
2. Establish data quality and ownership documentation for the supply
   chain domain
3. Deliver a foundation ready for future predictive supply chain use
   cases

## Initial Team
9-person team, primarily Cloud & Infrastructure and Data & Analytics,
with Daniel Haddad returning as Project Manager, carrying forward
relationship continuity with Horizon despite the 2022 engagement's early
conclusion.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Budget:** integration effort across the three source systems has
  some estimation uncertainty pending detailed technical scoping in
  week one.

## Initial Plan
Month 1: technical scoping across the three source systems. Months 2-6:
foundation build. Months 7-9: validation and governance handoff.
