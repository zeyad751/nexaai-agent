# Meeting Notes: Supply Chain Data Foundation — Mid-Project Check-in
**Date:** February 14, 2024
**Attendees:** Daniel Haddad (NexaAI PM), Adam Rizk (NexaAI, Cloud &
Infrastructure), Horizon Head of Supply Chain Planning

## Discussion
Daniel Haddad reported the foundation build was tracking ahead of plan,
with the supplier-data integration — expected to be the most complex of
the three source systems — turning out cleaner than the technical
scoping estimated. Adam Rizk noted the supply chain team's existing,
if informal, data ownership practices (each planner maintaining clear
notes on their supplier relationships) made reconciliation
straightforward compared to typical legacy-system consolidation work.

The Head of Supply Chain Planning raised, unprompted, that this
engagement's smooth progress stood in noticeable contrast to the
procurement automation engagement the year before, and asked whether
NexaAI had changed its approach. Daniel Haddad confirmed this directly:
this engagement is a backend data platform build with no front-line
workflow change required from planning staff, which removes the specific
adoption dependency that caused issues on the procurement engagement.

## Decision
No formal decision needed; team agreed to maintain current pace and use
the strong supplier-data integration results as a template for the
remaining production-scheduling integration.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to apply the supplier-data integration pattern to the
  production-scheduling system, given its similarity in structure
- Horizon to continue documenting informal data ownership notes as a
  standing practice going forward, independent of this engagement
