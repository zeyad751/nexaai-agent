# Post-Mortem: Enterprise Data Foundation for Supply Chain AI
**Date:** July 2024
**Status at close:** Completed 5 days ahead of schedule; actual cost
$438.3K against a $447.1K budget (slightly under)

## What Happened
A clean, strong delivery — the foundation was built on schedule and
under budget, with satisfaction climbing to 8.9/10 and adoption reaching
89.5% by close, among the strongest results on the Horizon account.

## What Worked
- The systems-level data maturity assessment conducted at proposal
  stage held up throughout — no significant data quality surprises
  emerged during the build, unlike the Delta Steel Manufacturing data
  platform engagement run under the same strategy around the same
  period.
- This engagement had no front-line workflow adoption dependency (a
  backend platform build, not a staff-facing process change), which is
  the structural reason it did not repeat the 2022 procurement
  engagement's failure mode on this same account.

## What Could Have Gone Better
Nothing significant flagged; this is one of the cleaner engagements in
the portfolio.

## Lessons Learned
This engagement, paired with the 2022 cancelled procurement automation
engagement on the same account, is a useful internal case study: the
client relationship itself was not the variable that mattered — the
presence or absence of a front-line staff adoption dependency was.
Two engagements with the same client can have completely different risk
profiles depending on whether the AI/automation touches how staff do
their daily work or operates entirely in the data backend.
