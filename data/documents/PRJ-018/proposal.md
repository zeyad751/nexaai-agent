# Project Proposal: Enterprise Data Foundation for Supply Chain AI
**Client:** Horizon Auto Components
**Date:** September 2023

## Business Problem
Horizon's supply chain planning team relies on manually reconciled data
from separate inventory, supplier, and production-scheduling systems,
limiting their ability to move toward predictive supply chain
capabilities.

## Proposed Solution
A **Data-First Transformation**: a governed Python/Snowflake data
foundation consolidating inventory, supplier, and production-scheduling
data ahead of future supply chain AI use cases.

## A Note on the Prior Engagement
NexaAI's 2022 procurement automation engagement with Horizon (RPA for
Procurement Workflows) concluded early due to front-line staff adoption
challenges rooted in informal, relationship-based workflow practices not
captured during requirements-gathering. This proposal is for a
structurally different kind of engagement — a backend data platform
build with no direct front-line workflow change for supply chain staff —
and we have specifically confirmed with Horizon's supply chain leadership
that this engagement does not carry a comparable adoption dependency. We
are noting this explicitly rather than treating the two engagements as
unrelated.

## Key Assumptions
A data maturity assessment of the inventory, supplier, and
production-scheduling systems in scope found them to be reasonably
well-structured, with an existing (if informal) data ownership model
NexaAI can build on rather than establish from scratch.

## Expected Outcomes
- A consolidated, governed data foundation for supply chain data
- Foundation for future predictive supply chain capabilities
- Improved reconciliation speed between inventory, supplier, and
  production data

## Strategy & Approach
Data-First Transformation is recommended here on the strength of the
systems-level maturity assessment described above, not on the basis of
the 2022 engagement's outcome (which is unrelated in scope and
maturity profile).

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $420K-$460K over an estimated 9-month engagement.
