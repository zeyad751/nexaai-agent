# Executive Review: Defect Detection Computer Vision Pilot (Cancelled)
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** April 2023
**Client:** AutoDrive Egypt | **Strategic Importance:** Low

## Summary
Cancelled early with minimal financial exposure ($49.9K spent against a
$159.1K budget). Root cause was a sponsorship-level gap — the pilot was
approved without consulting the frontline staff whose expertise it
touched most directly — resolved too late in the pilot's short timeline
to recover.

## Management Observations
This is the second cancelled engagement in the portfolio rooted in
organizational resistance (see also PRJ-017), and the two cases are
usefully distinct: PRJ-017's gap was leadership's description of a
process not matching how staff actually worked; this pilot's gap was
staff whose personal expertise was directly implicated never being
consulted before approval. Both fall under "organizational resistance"
as a risk category, but the underlying mechanism differs meaningfully.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend a specific proposal-stage question, distinct from general
stakeholder mapping: "does this initiative automate or evaluate a task
that a specific role has built deep personal expertise in, and if so,
has that role's practitioner — not just their manager — been consulted?"

## Open Items
AutoDrive has not indicated interest in revisiting computer vision
automation; this was AutoDrive's only engagement with NexaAI to date.
