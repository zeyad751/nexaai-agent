# Project Proposal: Defect Detection Computer Vision Pilot
**Client:** AutoDrive Egypt
**Date:** January 2023

## Business Problem
AutoDrive Egypt's assembly line relies entirely on manual visual
inspection for surface defect detection, a process line quality
engineers take pride in but that management believes could be
supplemented with automated detection for consistency.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: a computer vision defect-detection system tested
on a single production line, running alongside (not replacing) existing
manual inspection during the pilot period.

## Expected Outcomes
- Validate computer vision defect-detection accuracy against the
  existing manual inspection baseline
- Establish whether automated detection is a viable complement to
  manual inspection before any broader investment decision

## Key Assumptions
- Line quality engineers will cooperate with data collection (sample
  images, defect labeling) needed to train and validate the model
  during the pilot

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot is proposed specifically to test feasibility and
organizational fit on a single line before any larger commitment,
given this is AutoDrive's first AI engagement with NexaAI and
organizational appetite for this kind of automation is untested.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $150K-$165K over an estimated 3-month pilot.
