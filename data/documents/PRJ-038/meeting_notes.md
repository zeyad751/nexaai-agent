# Meeting Notes: Defect Detection Pilot — Data Collection Escalation
**Date:** March 20, 2023
**Attendees:** Bilal Baxter (NexaAI PM), Amr Hassan (NexaAI, AI
Engineering), AutoDrive Plant Manager, AutoDrive Line Quality Lead

## Discussion
Three weeks in, Bilal Baxter reported that image data collection was
far behind plan — line quality engineers had been slow to provide
labeled defect samples, and several requested sessions had been
cancelled or rescheduled by the quality team without clear reason.

The Line Quality Lead, pressed directly, was candid: the team had not
been consulted before the pilot was approved (sponsorship sat entirely
at the Plant Manager level), and quality engineers viewed the pilot as
an implicit judgment on the reliability of their manual inspection
process — a process several senior engineers had personally developed
and refined over years. Providing detailed defect labeling data felt, to
them, like helping build the case for replacing their own expertise,
and cooperation had been quietly deprioritized as a result.

The Plant Manager acknowledged not having brought the quality team into
the original proposal conversation, and that this was a gap in how the
pilot was introduced internally.

## Decision
NexaAI proposed a revised approach — reframing the pilot explicitly as a
complement to manual inspection with quality engineers named as
co-owners of the validation criteria, rather than data providers to a
system being evaluated to replace them. The Plant Manager and Line
Quality Lead discussed this but did not reach agreement within the
remaining budget and timeline for the pilot to meaningfully continue.

## Outcome
Both parties agreed to terminate the engagement. Logged as a
negative-outcome decision — the underlying organizational resistance,
rooted in how the pilot was introduced rather than in the technology
itself, was not resolvable within the pilot's remaining scope.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to document the sponsorship-level gap for future pilot
  proposals involving frontline technical staff whose expertise is
  adjacent to the proposed automation
