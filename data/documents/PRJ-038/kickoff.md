# Kickoff Document: Defect Detection Computer Vision Pilot
**Date:** February 25, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-038

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** AutoDrive Egypt, Plant Manager
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Bilal Baxter, Director of Project Management
- **NexaAI AI Engineering Lead:** Amr Hassan, Director of AI Engineering

## Objectives
1. Deploy a computer vision defect-detection model on a single
   production line
2. Validate detection accuracy against the manual inspection baseline
   over the pilot period
3. Produce a go/no-go recommendation for broader investment

## Initial Team
4-person team, intentionally minimal given the pilot's limited scope.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Timeline:** data collection depends on active cooperation from line
  quality engineers who were not directly consulted during proposal
  development (engagement was sponsored at the Plant Manager level);
  flagged as a risk to monitor.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-3: image data collection and initial model training. Weeks 4-8:
parallel-run validation against manual inspection. Weeks 9-12: results
review and go/no-go recommendation.
