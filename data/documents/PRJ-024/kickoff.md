# Kickoff Document: Enterprise AI Transformation Program
**Date:** January 24, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-024

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Nour Telecom CEO and CTO (joint program governance)
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Ryan Shawky
- **NexaAI Strategy Lead:** Karim Baxter, Director of Strategy Consulting

## Objectives
1. Extend customer analytics capabilities beyond the initial churn
   prediction pilot
2. Establish shared AI infrastructure for network operations and
   customer-facing use cases
3. Maintain sustained executive sponsorship and program momentum across
   the full multi-year timeline

## Initial Team
21-person team, the largest single team in the current portfolio,
spanning AI Engineering, Data & Analytics, Cloud & Infrastructure,
Strategy Consulting, and Finance.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** integrating multiple use cases onto shared
  infrastructure while maintaining each use case's independent delivery
  timeline is a coordination challenge; flagged for close program
  management attention.

## Initial Plan
Phase 1 (2023): customer analytics expansion and shared infrastructure
foundation. Phase 2 (early 2024): network operations use cases and
customer-facing capability pilots.
