# Post-Mortem: Enterprise AI Transformation Program (Phase 1)
**Date:** April 2024
**Status at close:** Completed essentially on schedule; actual cost
$1.04M against a $1.01M budget (~3% over, minor)

## What Happened
Phase 1 delivered strong, broad-based results: satisfaction climbed from
6.9 to 8.9 and adoption from 34% to nearly 89% by close. ROI started
very high (114.6% in the first reporting quarter, reflecting early,
low-cost wins from the churn-prediction foundation) and moderated to a
still-excellent 68.3% as the program scaled — the same front-loaded ROI
pattern seen on the Meridian Fraud Detection engagement, not a
performance concern.

## What Worked
- Sustained CEO/CTO-level sponsorship, present from kickoff through the
  Phase 1-to-2 transition decision, meant program-level decisions (like
  the July 2023 "validate before scaling" choice) were made quickly and
  with full authority, rather than requiring escalation.
- The technology sector's comparatively lighter regulatory review burden
  meant delivery timelines were not subject to the compliance review
  gates that materially affected similar-scale engagements at Meridian
  National Bank and government accounts — worth naming explicitly as a
  structural, not effort-based, advantage.

## What Could Have Gone Better
Nothing significant; this is one of the strongest results in the
portfolio.

## Lessons Learned
This program is worth contrasting directly with the Enterprise AI
Transformation engagements run under the same strategy at Meridian
National Bank (PRJ-002, PRJ-005 pattern) and Gulf Public Services
Authority: the strategy itself performed comparably well in technical
execution across all these accounts, but industry regulatory profile
was the single largest differentiator in schedule and budget outcomes.
