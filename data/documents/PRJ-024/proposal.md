# Project Proposal: Enterprise AI Transformation Program
**Client:** Nour Telecom
**Date:** November 2022

## Business Problem
Following a successful 2022 churn prediction pilot (PRJ-023), Nour
Telecom's executive leadership has committed to a broader, multi-year AI
transformation spanning customer analytics, network operations, and
customer-facing capabilities.

## Proposed Solution
An **Enterprise AI Transformation** engagement: a multi-phase program
building on the churn prediction foundation, extending into additional
customer analytics use cases and establishing shared AI infrastructure
for future capabilities across the business.

## Expected Outcomes
- Multiple production AI capabilities delivered across a single
  coordinated program rather than disconnected point projects
- Reusable AI infrastructure reducing marginal cost of future use cases
- Sustained executive sponsorship maintained across the full multi-year
  program

## Key Assumptions
- Nour Telecom's executive sponsorship (CEO and CTO both directly
  engaged in program governance) will remain stable across the
  multi-year timeline
- As a telecommunications provider, this program's use cases (customer
  analytics, network operations) do not carry the same regulatory review
  burden that similar programs face in banking, insurance, or government
  accounts — this is treated as a favorable, if implicit, scoping
  assumption

## Strategy & Approach
Enterprise AI Transformation is recommended specifically because Nour's
leadership has committed board-level, multi-year budget and sustained
executive sponsorship — the precondition this strategy depends on.
Unlike similar-scale engagements in regulated industries, this program's
delivery plan does not build in extended compliance review checkpoints
as a structural element, given the lighter regulatory profile of the use
cases in scope.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $950K-$1.05M over an estimated 15-month first phase.
