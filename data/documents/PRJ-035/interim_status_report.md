# Interim Status Report: Government Data Platform Modernization
**Date:** September 2025 (project ongoing)
**Status:** In Progress — Production Go-Live and Project Closeout
milestones not yet started; planned completion November 2025

## Current Position
As of the most recent reporting period, cumulative cost stands at
$734.8K against a $1.06M budget, with $395.2K in revenue recognized to
date. ROI on a cumulative basis remains negative (-46.2% most recently),
which is expected at this stage — recognized revenue lags cost on a
platform-level engagement where most billable value is tied to the
production go-live milestone, still ahead. Adoption among pilot
departments is climbing steadily (23.6% to 41.8% across the reporting
periods to date), and satisfaction has held in the moderate 5.0-5.4 range
throughout.

## What Has Happened So Far
The inter-ministerial compliance review process (see November 2024
meeting notes) extended the original schedule as anticipated once
confirmed, but has not introduced further surprises since — the
extended review is proceeding within the revised timeline agreed at that
session.

## Open Risk
The Regulatory risk logged in November 2024 remains formally open
(status: Realized, not yet resolved) — the inter-ministerial approval
process is still in progress as of this report. This is being tracked
against the Production Go-Live milestone, currently the next scheduled
milestone.

## Outlook
Assuming the compliance review concludes on its currently projected
timeline, Production Go-Live and Project Closeout are expected to proceed
as re-baselined. A full post-mortem will be produced at project close.
