# Executive Review: Government Data Platform Modernization (In Progress)
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** September 2025
**Client:** Ministry of Digital Development | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
Currently on track against its re-baselined schedule following the
November 2024 compliance-scope extension, with no further surprises
since. Cumulative ROI remains negative, which is expected given the
platform-level billing structure and is not a current concern.

## Management Observations
This is now the third confirmed instance of the "broader-scope
initiative triggers proportionally more complex compliance review, not
just a longer version of a narrower review" pattern, following PRJ-003
(Meridian) and PRJ-031 (Trustline). Here it was caught proactively at
kickoff rather than mid-delivery, likely because the pattern is now
recognized enough internally to flag early — worth confirming this is
becoming standard practice rather than a one-off catch.

## Strategic Implications
This engagement is a good candidate for validating whether the "flag the
compliance-scope-parity risk explicitly at kickoff" practice, applied
here, actually produces better outcomes than the reactive escalations
seen on PRJ-003 and PRJ-031 — worth a comparison once this project
closes.
