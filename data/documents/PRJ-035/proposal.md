# Project Proposal: Government Data Platform Modernization
**Client:** Ministry of Digital Development
**Date:** July 2024

## Business Problem
Following the Ministry's 2022-2023 digital services initiatives (see
PRJ-032, PRJ-033), citizen-facing data remains fragmented across
multiple legacy government systems, limiting the Ministry's ability to
deliver unified digital services.

## Proposed Solution
An **Enterprise AI Transformation** engagement establishing a governed
Azure/Snowflake data platform consolidating citizen-facing service data
across the Ministry's legacy systems.

## Expected Outcomes
- A unified, governed data platform underpinning future digital
  government services
- Foundation for cross-department data sharing within the Ministry
- Improved data quality and consistency for citizen-facing services

## Key Assumptions
- The Ministry's continued digital transformation mandate and executive
  sponsorship (established across the prior two engagements) will remain
  stable
- As with prior Ministry engagements, public-sector procurement and data
  sovereignty review requirements will apply and are budgeted for, though
  the specific scope of review for a platform-level (versus
  service-level) initiative has not been independently confirmed with
  the Ministry's legal function

## Strategy & Approach
Enterprise AI Transformation reflects the multi-year, board-level nature
of the Ministry's broader digital transformation mandate. Given this is
the Ministry's third engagement with NexaAI, sustained executive
sponsorship is treated as a lower risk than on a new account; the
platform-level compliance review scope is treated as the primary open
question.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $1.0M-$1.1M over an estimated 14-month engagement.
