# Meeting Notes: Data Platform Modernization — Compliance Scope Review
**Date:** November 25, 2024
**Attendees:** Karim Youssef (NexaAI PM), Ali Bishop (NexaAI, Data &
Analytics), Ministry Deputy Minister for Digital Transformation, Ministry
Legal Affairs Director

## Discussion
As flagged at kickoff, the team brought the platform-level compliance
review question to Ministry Legal early. The Legal Affairs Director
confirmed that a platform consolidating data across multiple government
systems triggers a more extensive data sovereignty and inter-departmental
data-sharing review than either of the Ministry's two prior,
narrower-scoped digital services engagements required — each of those
touched a single service domain, while this platform spans several.

The Deputy Minister acknowledged this was expected to some degree given
the platform's broader scope, but the specific review timeline (a
formal inter-ministerial data-sharing approval process) was longer than
either party had initially estimated.

Karim Youssef noted this mirrors a pattern NexaAI has seen on other
regulated-industry and government engagements: assuming a new,
broader-scope initiative's compliance requirements are proportional to a
narrower prior engagement's requirements, when in practice the review
process itself scales in complexity, not just duration.

## Decision
The team agreed to insert a formal compliance review gate reflecting the
confirmed inter-ministerial approval process, extending the original
schedule. Logged as a mixed-outcome decision — the earlier proposal-stage
flag meant this was surfaced proactively rather than discovered as a
surprise later, but it still represents a real cost to the original plan.

## Action Items
- NexaAI to prepare the inter-ministerial data-sharing documentation
  package required for the extended review
- Ministry Legal to initiate the formal approval process in parallel
  with continued platform build work
