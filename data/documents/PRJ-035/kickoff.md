# Kickoff Document: Government Data Platform Modernization
**Date:** September 10, 2024
**Project ID:** PRJ-035

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Ministry of Digital Development, Deputy Minister
  for Digital Transformation
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Karim Youssef
- **NexaAI Data & Analytics Lead:** Ali Bishop, Data Scientist Lead

## Objectives
1. Consolidate citizen-facing service data across legacy Ministry
   systems into a governed platform
2. Establish cross-department data-sharing capability within the
   Ministry
3. Confirm the platform-level compliance review scope with Ministry
   legal early in delivery

## Initial Team
13-person team, primarily Data & Analytics and Strategy Consulting, with
Cybersecurity representation for data sovereignty considerations.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Timeline:** the platform-level compliance review scope flagged at
  proposal stage as unconfirmed is the primary risk to resolve early.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: technical discovery and compliance review scope
confirmation. Months 4-10: platform build. Months 11-14: migration and
governance handoff.
