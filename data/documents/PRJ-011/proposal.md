# Project Proposal: Demand Forecasting Platform
**Client:** Cedar Retail Group
**Date:** December 2022

## Business Problem
Cedar Retail's inventory planning relies on simple historical-average
forecasting that does not account for seasonality, promotions, or
regional demand variation, leading to frequent overstock and stockout
situations.

## Proposed Solution
A **Build-Measure-Learn** engagement: an iterative demand forecasting
platform, starting with a minimum viable model and refining based on
real forecast-accuracy feedback.

## Expected Outcomes
- Improved forecast accuracy versus current historical-average methods
- Reduced overstock and stockout incidents

## Strategy & Approach
Build-Measure-Learn is proposed given Cedar Retail's openness to
iterative delivery, demonstrated on the earlier Personalized
Recommendation Engine engagement.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $205K-$225K over an estimated 9-month engagement.
