# Kickoff Document: Demand Forecasting Platform
**Date:** January 23, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-011

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Cedar Retail Group, Head of Inventory Planning
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Mostafa Rashad

## Objectives
1. Deploy a minimum viable demand forecasting model
2. Iterate based on real forecast-accuracy feedback each cycle
3. Reduce overstock/stockout incidents versus current methods

## Initial Team
Data & Analytics-led team, moderate size given the iterative delivery
approach.

## Initial Plan
Iterative build-measure-learn cycles across the engagement, with a first
minimum viable model targeted within the initial 2 months.
