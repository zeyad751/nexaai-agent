# Executive Review: National Digital Services AI Roadmap
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** January 2023
**Client:** Ministry of Digital Development | **Strategic Importance:** High

## Summary
Completed roughly on schedule (14 days over the original plan) with a
moderate cost overrun (~15%, $3.24M against $2.82M budget), driven
primarily by a Talent risk — a key team member's mid-engagement
departure required backfill and knowledge transfer. Satisfaction and
overall score both improved modestly across the engagement (5.9 to 6.5;
48.9 to 55.0) — steady, unremarkable progress consistent with a
first-phase roadmap-setting engagement rather than a flagship win.

## Management Observations
This is the first of what is now a multi-year, multi-project
relationship with the Ministry (see also PRJ-034, PRJ-035). Financial
performance across this account has been consistently modest rather than
strong across all three engagements to date, even as the relationship
itself continues and the Ministry has expressed sustained interest in
further phases — worth noting as a pattern distinct from purely
financial account prioritization.

## Strategic Implications
The Incremental Modernization strategy performed as intended here — no
major disruption, steady if modest progress — which is the right
benchmark for this strategy rather than expecting flagship-level ROI.

## Open Items
Ministry has committed to further phases; see PRJ-034 (Citizen Service
Chatbot) and PRJ-035 (Data Platform Modernization) for subsequent
engagements under this ongoing relationship.
