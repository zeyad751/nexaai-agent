# Project Proposal: National Digital Services AI Roadmap
**Client:** Ministry of Digital Development
**Date:** October 2021

## Business Problem
The Ministry's digital services span multiple legacy systems built over
different administrations, with no coordinated roadmap for introducing
AI capabilities across citizen-facing services.

## Proposed Solution
An **Incremental Modernization** engagement: a phased AI roadmap
introducing capabilities in small, low-risk increments across the
Ministry's digital services, rather than a single large transformation.

## Expected Outcomes
- A phased, low-risk roadmap for AI adoption across Ministry digital
  services
- Early wins building institutional confidence for further phases

## Key Assumptions
- The Ministry's mission-critical legacy systems require a
  low-disruption modernization approach given public-service continuity
  requirements

## Strategy & Approach
Incremental Modernization is recommended specifically because the
Ministry's systems are mission-critical public services that cannot
tolerate the disruption risk of a single large transformation — this is
a deliberately risk-averse sequencing choice given the public-sector
context.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $2.7M-$2.9M over an estimated 11-month first phase.
