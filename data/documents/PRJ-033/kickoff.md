# Kickoff Document: National Digital Services AI Roadmap
**Date:** January 4, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-033

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Ministry of Digital Development, Deputy Minister
  for Digital Transformation
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Aya Fahmy

## Objectives
1. Deliver a phased AI capability roadmap across Ministry digital services
2. Demonstrate early wins to build institutional confidence for further
   phases
3. Establish this as the foundation for the Ministry's ongoing digital
   transformation relationship with NexaAI

## Initial Team
14-person team spanning AI Engineering, Data & Analytics, and Strategy
Consulting.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Budget:** phased public-sector engagements can face budget-cycle
  timing dependencies; flagged for early alignment with the Ministry's
  fiscal year planning.

## Initial Plan
Phase 1 (2022): roadmap definition and first low-risk capability
increment. Subsequent phases to be scoped based on Phase 1 results and
Ministry fiscal planning.
