# Project Proposal: Chatbot for Policy Inquiries Pilot
**Client:** Nile Assurance Co.
**Date:** August 2022

## Business Problem
Nile Assurance's customer service line handles a high volume of routine
policy inquiry calls (coverage questions, renewal dates, premium
amounts) that consume agent capacity needed for more complex claims
conversations.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: a generative AI chatbot handling routine policy
inquiries via the customer web portal, tested before any broader
customer service automation investment.

## Expected Outcomes
- Deflection of routine policy inquiries away from phone agents
- A go/no-go basis for broader customer service automation

## Key Assumptions
- Policy documentation is structured well enough to ground chatbot
  responses accurately with moderate setup effort

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot given this is Nile Assurance's first generative AI
engagement and organizational readiness for customer-facing automation
is untested.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $165K-$180K over an estimated 2-month pilot.
