# Client Feedback: Chatbot for Policy Inquiries Pilot
**Client:** Nile Assurance Co.
**Collected:** December 2022, end-of-pilot review

## Feedback Summary
"Better than I expected for something built this quickly. Customers
seemed comfortable using it for the simple questions, which is exactly
what we wanted it for."
— Head of Customer Service, Nile Assurance Co.

## Additional Notes
The Head of Customer Service noted Nile Assurance is a smaller insurer
relative to some of NexaAI's other Insurance accounts, and that
customer-facing automation beyond this pilot would be a board-level
technology investment decision rather than something Customer Service
can commit to independently. No timeline was indicated for that broader
decision.

## NexaAI Internal Note
Strong satisfaction result (7.8 rising to 8.4) on Nile Assurance's first
generative AI engagement. Follow-on, if any, depends on a board-level
decision outside the sponsoring department's authority — a smaller-
account variant of the governance-level gating seen on a few other
pilots in the portfolio.
