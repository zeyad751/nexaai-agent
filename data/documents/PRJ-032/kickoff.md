# Kickoff Document: Chatbot for Policy Inquiries Pilot
**Date:** October 19, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-032

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Nile Assurance Co., Head of Customer Service
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Daniel Haddad

## Objectives
1. Deploy a chatbot handling routine policy inquiries on the web portal
2. Measure inquiry deflection rate away from phone agents
3. Produce a broader automation rollout recommendation

## Initial Team
4-person team, lean given the pilot scope.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-3: policy documentation grounding and chatbot configuration.
Weeks 4-8: live pilot on the web portal and deflection measurement.
