# Client Feedback: Network Anomaly Detection Pilot
**Client:** Zenith Mobile UAE
**Collected:** September 2023, end-of-pilot review

## Feedback Summary
"It caught two degradation patterns our current alerting would have
missed until customers started complaining. That's a real result for
such a short pilot."
— Head of Network Operations, Zenith Mobile UAE

## Additional Notes
The Head of Network Operations indicated interest in a network-wide
rollout but noted that Zenith Mobile's parent group runs a centralized
annual technology investment review, and any infrastructure-wide
monitoring change would need to go through that group-level process
rather than being approved at the local network operations level. No
timeline was available for when that review might consider this pilot's
results.

## NexaAI Internal Note
Very strong satisfaction result (7.4 rising to 8.4) on a minimal
3-person pilot team. The lack of follow-on to date reflects a group-level
governance/budget process outside local operations' control, not pilot
performance.
