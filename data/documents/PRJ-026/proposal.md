# Project Proposal: Network Anomaly Detection Pilot
**Client:** Zenith Mobile UAE
**Date:** May 2023

## Business Problem
Zenith Mobile's network operations team currently relies on threshold-
based alerting for network anomalies, which misses gradual degradation
patterns that don't cross a fixed threshold until service is already
affected.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: an anomaly-detection model tested on a subset of
network infrastructure, designed to catch gradual degradation patterns
earlier than threshold-based alerting.

## Expected Outcomes
- Earlier detection of network degradation versus current
  threshold-based alerting
- A go/no-go basis for network-wide rollout

## Key Assumptions
- Historical network performance data for the pilot infrastructure
  subset is sufficient for anomaly model training

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot given the value of validating detection improvement on a
limited infrastructure subset before any larger commitment.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $160K-$180K over an estimated 2-month pilot.
