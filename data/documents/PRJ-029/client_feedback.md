# Client Feedback: Predictive Equipment Failure Pilot
**Client:** Delta Petrol Egypt
**Collected:** March 2024, end-of-pilot review

## Feedback Summary
"Our maintenance team was skeptical going in, but the early-warning
flags matched what our senior technicians would have caught anyway —
and caught one thing they didn't. That built real trust quickly."
— Head of Maintenance, Delta Petrol Egypt

## Additional Notes
The Head of Maintenance expressed clear interest in expanding to
additional equipment categories, but noted that Delta Petrol's capital
planning cycle for the following year had already been finalized before
the pilot concluded, and any expansion would need to wait for the next
annual planning cycle.

## NexaAI Internal Note
Strong satisfaction result (7.2 rising to 8.2) with genuine technical
buy-in from initially skeptical maintenance staff — a notable contrast to
some other pilots where adoption enthusiasm outpaced technical
skepticism resolution. Follow-on timing is governed by the client's
annual capital planning cycle.
