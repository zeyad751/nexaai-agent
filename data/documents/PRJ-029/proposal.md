# Project Proposal: Predictive Equipment Failure Pilot
**Client:** Delta Petrol Egypt
**Date:** September 2023

## Business Problem
Delta Petrol's refinery equipment maintenance is currently reactive,
with no predictive capability for equipment failure risk.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: a predictive maintenance model tested on a single
equipment category, to validate feasibility before broader investment.

## Expected Outcomes
- Validated early-warning capability for the pilot equipment category
- A go/no-go basis for broader refinery-wide rollout

## Key Assumptions
- Existing sensor data for the pilot equipment category is sufficient
  for model training

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot given this is Delta Petrol's first AI engagement with
NexaAI and organizational appetite is untested.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $75K-$85K over an estimated 4-month pilot.
