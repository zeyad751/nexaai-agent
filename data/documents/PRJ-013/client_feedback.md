# Client Feedback: In-Store Analytics Pilot
**Client:** BasketWay Egypt
**Collected:** August 2023, end-of-pilot review

## Feedback Summary
"The accuracy was better than we expected, honestly — the dwell-time
numbers matched our manual spot-checks closely enough that we trust the
system for the flagship store. The team from NexaAI was easy to work
with and the pilot ran smoothly from our side."
— Head of Store Operations, BasketWay Egypt

## Additional Notes
The Head of Store Operations noted that a wider rollout to BasketWay's
remaining stores is "something we'd like to revisit," but that store-
level technology spend for the next fiscal year has already been
allocated to a separate point-of-sale system upgrade. No firm timeline
was given for reconsidering a multi-store rollout.

## NexaAI Internal Note
Satisfaction scores for this pilot were among the highest in the
portfolio (7.4 rising to 8.4). The absence of a follow-on engagement
reflects BasketWay's separate, already-committed technology budget
priorities rather than any concern with pilot results.
