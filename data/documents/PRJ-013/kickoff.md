# Kickoff Document: In-Store Analytics Pilot
**Date:** June 5, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-013

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** BasketWay Egypt, Head of Store Operations
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Bilal Baxter, Director of Project Management

## Objectives
1. Deploy foot-traffic and dwell-time analytics on the flagship store
2. Validate measurement accuracy against manual spot-checks
3. Produce a rollout recommendation for additional stores

## Initial Team
4-person team, lean given the single-store pilot scope.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-2: camera infrastructure assessment and model calibration. Weeks
3-8: live measurement and validation against manual counts.
