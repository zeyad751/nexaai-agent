# Project Proposal: In-Store Analytics Pilot
**Client:** BasketWay Egypt
**Date:** May 2023

## Business Problem
BasketWay wants to understand in-store customer traffic patterns but has
no current instrumentation beyond point-of-sale data.

## Proposed Solution
A **Rapid AI Pilot**: computer-vision-based foot traffic and dwell-time
analytics on a single flagship store, to test feasibility before any
wider rollout.

## Expected Outcomes
- Validated foot-traffic and dwell-time measurement accuracy
- A go/no-go basis for wider store rollout

## Key Assumptions
- Existing in-store camera infrastructure is sufficient for computer
  vision analytics with minimal additional hardware

## Strategy & Approach
Rapid AI Pilot given BasketWay's limited prior AI investment and the
value of testing feasibility on one store before any larger commitment.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $110K-$125K over an estimated 2-month pilot.
