# Meeting Notes: Recommendation Engine — Mid-Rollout Business Review
**Date:** October 5, 2022
**Attendees:** Bilal Baxter (NexaAI PM), Amr Hassan (NexaAI, AI
Engineering), Cedar Retail Head of Customer Experience, Cedar Retail
Finance Business Partner

## Discussion
Bilal Baxter opened with strong engagement numbers from the cart-page
pilot: click-through on recommended items was well above the
best-seller-baseline, and qualitative customer feedback captured through
CX's usual channels was notably positive — several customer comments
specifically praised "relevant" recommendations for the first time.

The Cedar Retail Finance Business Partner, joining for the first time
since kickoff, asked a direct question the room did not have a fully
prepared answer for: what is the actual incremental revenue attributable
to the new recommendations, net of the additional infrastructure and
tuning cost being carried? The Head of Customer Experience acknowledged
that the original business case had been framed around engagement and
satisfaction metrics rather than a specific revenue-attribution model,
and that isolating incremental revenue cleanly would require additional
A/B testing infrastructure not currently in scope.

Amr Hassan noted that infrastructure costs were tracking slightly above
the original estimate due to additional tuning cycles needed for
lower-traffic product categories (the cold-start issue flagged at
kickoff), which — combined with the unresolved revenue-attribution
question — meant the project's satisfaction story was outpacing its
demonstrable financial story.

## Decision
No scope change was made; the team agreed to continue the current
rollout plan. The Finance Business Partner's question was logged as an
open item rather than answered definitively, since building proper
revenue-attribution tooling was assessed as a separate, unscoped
initiative.

## Action Items
- CX team to continue tracking engagement metrics as the primary success
  measure per the original business case framing
- Finance to consider whether a future phase should include dedicated
  A/B revenue-attribution infrastructure
