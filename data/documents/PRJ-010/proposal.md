# Project Proposal: Personalized Recommendation Engine
**Client:** Cedar Retail Group
**Date:** February 2022

## Business Problem
Cedar Retail's e-commerce platform currently shows the same
best-seller-based recommendations to every customer, regardless of
browsing or purchase history. Customer experience feedback has
repeatedly flagged recommendations as "irrelevant" or "generic."

## Proposed Solution
A **Customer-Centric AI** engagement: a personalized recommendation
engine using collaborative filtering and browsing-behavior signals,
integrated into the product listing and cart pages.

## Expected Outcomes
- Substantially more relevant, individualized product recommendations
- Improved customer engagement metrics (click-through and time-on-page)
  on recommendation-driven surfaces
- A foundation for future personalization use cases (email, search
  ranking) beyond this initial scope

## Key Assumptions
- Sufficient browsing and purchase history data exists to support
  collaborative filtering at reasonable quality from day one
- Success will be primarily evaluated through customer engagement and
  satisfaction metrics, consistent with this being a customer-experience-
  led initiative championed by the marketing and CX teams

## Strategy & Approach
This is proposed and scoped as a Customer-Centric AI initiative, driven
by customer experience research rather than a hard financial target.
Cedar's marketing team is the primary sponsor; Finance is a secondary
stakeholder. We note this framing explicitly because it shapes what
"success" means for this engagement — the business case here is
built around engagement and satisfaction outcomes rather than a specific
revenue-uplift target.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $620K-$660K over an estimated 10-month engagement.
