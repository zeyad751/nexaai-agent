# Executive Review: Personalized Recommendation Engine
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** February 2023
**Client:** Cedar Retail Group | **Strategic Importance:** High

## Summary
A useful counter-example to keep in view alongside our higher-ROI
projects: strong customer satisfaction (8.5/10 at close) and adoption
(63%) paired with only modest, flat ROI (~14%). This was not a delivery
shortfall — it reflects how the business case was framed from the start,
plus a real (if modest) cost overrun from cold-start tuning that had no
revenue metric available to offset it in reporting.

## Management Observations
Customer-Centric AI engagements led primarily by CX/Marketing
stakeholders, as this one was, are prone to this exact pattern: strong
qualitative and engagement outcomes without the infrastructure to
demonstrate a clean financial return, even when a real one likely exists.
Finance was a secondary stakeholder here from the outset, which shows in
how the mid-project revenue-attribution question (October 2022 meeting)
went unresolved rather than answered.

## Strategic Implications
For future Customer-Centric AI proposals, recommend including a minimal
revenue-attribution mechanism (e.g., basic A/B holdout measurement) as
standard scope regardless of whether Finance is a primary sponsor — the
cost of adding it upfront is small relative to the cost of a genuinely
successful project being unable to demonstrate its own financial case
later.
