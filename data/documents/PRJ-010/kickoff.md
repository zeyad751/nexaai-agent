# Kickoff Document: Personalized Recommendation Engine
**Date:** March 17, 2022
**Project ID:** PRJ-010

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Cedar Retail Group, Head of Customer Experience
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Bilal Baxter, Director of Project Management
- **NexaAI AI Engineering Lead:** Amr Hassan, Director of AI Engineering

## Objectives
1. Deploy individualized product recommendations on listing and cart pages
2. Improve click-through and engagement metrics versus the current
   best-seller-based baseline
3. Establish reusable personalization infrastructure for future use cases

## Initial Team
8-person team, lean by design given the well-scoped nature of the use
case — primarily AI Engineering and Data Science, with Project
Management continuity from Bilal Baxter throughout.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** cold-start recommendation quality for lower-traffic
  product categories is a known open question, to be addressed during
  model iteration.

## Initial Plan
Months 1-3: model development against historical browsing/purchase data.
Months 4-6: pilot on the cart page only. Months 7-10: full rollout to
listing pages and iterative tuning based on live engagement data.
