# Project Proposal: Data Platform Modernization
**Client:** Delta Steel Manufacturing
**Date:** January 2023

## Business Problem
Beyond the plant-floor sensor data used in the 2022 Predictive Maintenance
engagement, Delta Steel's broader enterprise data — ERP, procurement,
finance, and quality-control records — remains fragmented across legacy
systems with no unified governance, limiting the company's ability to
extend AI capabilities beyond the plant floor.

## Proposed Solution
A **Data-First Transformation**: consolidating Delta Steel's enterprise
data into a governed Databricks/Snowflake foundation ahead of any new
AI use cases in this domain, following the same strategic sequencing
used successfully in the Predictive Maintenance engagement's underlying
data pipeline.

## Key Assumptions
Given the strong data foundation established for plant-floor sensor data
in the 2022 engagement, we are assuming Delta Steel's broader enterprise
data — ERP, finance, procurement — is at a comparable maturity level, and
that the same central IT governance authority that supported the
Centralized AI Platform pilot extends cleanly to these enterprise
systems. **This assumption has not been independently verified against
the specific enterprise systems in scope** — it is inferred from the
prior engagement's success in an adjacent but distinct part of Delta
Steel's data landscape (operational sensor data, not enterprise records).

## Expected Outcomes
- A single governed enterprise data foundation replacing the current
  fragmented legacy systems
- Foundation for extending AI capabilities from the plant floor into
  procurement, finance, and quality domains

## Strategy & Approach
Scoped as a direct,6-month sequenced build given the confidence carried
over from the 2022 engagement's success. We note internally that this is
a faster-than-usual data maturity validation than we would normally
recommend for an engagement of this scope and financial exposure, given
the strength of the prior relationship and results.

## Indicative Investment
Estimated budget: $2.05M-$2.2M over an estimated 14-month engagement.
