# Kickoff Document: Data Platform Modernization
**Date:** March 5, 2023
**Project ID:** PRJ-015

## Stakeholders
- **Client sponsor:** Delta Steel Manufacturing, Group CIO
- **NexaAI Project Manager:** Karim Youssef
- **NexaAI Data & Analytics Lead:** Ahmed Qureshi, Senior Data Scientist

## Objectives
1. Consolidate ERP, procurement, finance, and quality-control data into
   a governed Databricks/Snowflake foundation
2. Extend the Centralized AI Platform's governance model into these
   enterprise domains
3. Establish a foundation for future AI use cases beyond plant-floor
   operations

## Initial Team
13-person team, primarily Data & Analytics and Strategy Consulting, with
Finance and Cybersecurity representation added given the sensitivity of
the financial and procurement data domains in scope.

## Known Risks at Kickoff
- **Technical:** integration complexity across four distinct legacy
  enterprise systems (ERP, procurement, finance, quality) is
  acknowledged as higher than the single-domain sensor pipeline handled
  in the 2022 engagement; flagged for early technical discovery.

## Initial Plan
Weeks 1-3: technical discovery across the four target systems. Months
2-8: foundation build. Months 9-14: migration, validation, and
governance handoff.
