# Executive Review: Data Platform Modernization
**Prepared for:** NexaAI Solutions Leadership Team
**Date:** July 2024
**Client:** Delta Steel Manufacturing | **Strategic Importance:** Critical

## Summary
A significant overrun (~26% over budget, ~43 days late against the
revised plan) traced to a single, specific, and avoidable cause: the
proposal generalized data maturity from one successful but unrelated
engagement (2022 plant-floor sensor pipeline) to a different set of
enterprise systems (ERP, procurement) without independently verifying it.
Financially recovered somewhat by close (ROI reached near-breakeven from
a deeply negative start) but this remains one of the weaker outcomes on
the Delta Steel account.

## Management Observations
This is the clearest internal example yet of a specific, nameable failure
pattern: treating "we know this client" as equivalent to "we have
verified this specific system's data maturity." The Falcon Gulf Bank
engagement (PRJ-004), run under the identical Data-First Transformation
strategy in roughly the same period, shows the strategy performing well
when the maturity assessment is done properly — this is a process gap on
this specific engagement, not a strategy weakness.

## Strategic Implications
Recommend a firm-wide proposal review checkpoint: any engagement that
proposes skipping or abbreviating a systems-level data maturity
assessment on the basis of "we've delivered successfully for this client
before" should require an explicit sign-off noting the specific prior
system(s) verified and confirming the new system(s) in scope are
genuinely comparable — not just organizationally adjacent.
